 Janssen Pharmaceuticals, Inc. v. Kennedy
 Certiorari Denied Petition for certiorari denied on May 18, 2026 .

 Docket No. 25-749 

 Op. Below 3d Cir. 

 SCOTUS Page 
 Issue
 Whether the Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program (opens in new tab) , which imposes new requirements for certain widely prescribed drugs: (1) violates the First Amendment by compelling Janssen to express the government’s messages regarding drug pricing; (2) effects a Fifth Amendment taking by forcing Janssen to transfer its Xarelto® products to third parties on the government’s terms; and (3) is immune from constitutional scrutiny because it secures compliance through economic coercion.
