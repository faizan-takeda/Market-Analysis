 Selected Drugs and Negotiated Prices 

 The file below contains the list of National Drug Codes (NDC) associated with the selected drug list, which represents the NDCs for which any negotiated maximum fair price would apply consistent with CMS guidance. 
 For any selected drugs for which CMS and participating drug companies have agreed upon negotiated prices under the Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program, which the statute refers to as maximum fair prices (MFPs) the file also contains the single negotiated price for a 30-day equivalent supply of each selected drug, NDC-9 per unit price, and NDC-11 per package price, as well as other important information regarding updates to the NDCs and MFPs.  

 The file is updated annually each year subsequent to the first initial price applicability year of the price applicability period for each selected drug, at a minimum, to show the inflation-adjusted negotiated price for the selected drug. Additional updates are made on a recurring basis to account for the application of the negotiated price to new NDC-11s of selected drugs or when an NDC-11 should no longer have the negotiated price applied. Lastly, additional updates are made to account for when a drug is no longer selected. CMS will publish the file in both .xlsx and .csv file formats.

 Drugs with Deselection Determinations
 A selected drug will no longer be subject to the negotiation process and will cease to be a selected drug under certain circumstances, such as when FDA has approved a generic drug that identifies as its reference-listed drug a product that is included in the selected drug, or FDA has licensed a biosimilar that identifies as its reference product a product that is included in the selected drug, and the generic drug or biosimilar, as applicable, is marketed pursuant to such approval or licensure. Section 70 of Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program: Final Guidance, Implementation of Sections 1191 – 1198 of the Social Security Act for Initial Price Applicability Year 2028 and Manufacturer Effectuation of the Maximum Fair Price in 2026, 2027, and 2028 (“ Final Guidance (PDF) ”) details the determination process and timeline of removal for deselected drugs from the Selected Drug List and Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program.
 When CMS makes a deselection determination, CMS issues a memo through the CMS Health Plan Management System (CMS HPMS) notifying affected plans and stakeholders.
