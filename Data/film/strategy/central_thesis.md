# TAKEDA 2040 — central thesis of the film

*Gate 1 document. Written before the screenplay, from the MEDICINE 2040 research only (strategy v2, council record, evidence registry). No new research.*

## The one sentence

**No single drug, deal or technology secures Takeda's next decade; what endures is an organisation that discovers repeatedly, measures honestly, connects evidence to patients, and learns faster than the market moves — so Takeda should defend what is dated and build what repeats, starting in 2027, deciding at dated gates.**

## Why this and not a product story

1. **Takeda's exposure is dated and quantified** (FACT): Entyvio is JPY 958.0bn, 21% of FY2025 revenue [CLM-005009]; CMS selected it for a negotiated price effective January 2028 [CLM-007026]; two vedolizumab biosimilar applications are under FDA review [CLM-014051]; Vyvanse fell JPY 220bn in two years after generic entry [CLM-005015]. The erosion range on screen (JPY 383–766bn/yr) is a **SCENARIO** derived from ASM-024, never a forecast.
2. **The competitor lessons are mechanisms, not targets to copy** (INTERPRETATION): Lilly shows concentration creates price and payer exposure before LOE [CLM-000133, CLM-000137, CLM-001003, CLM-014003]; AbbVie shows the successor must already be marketed and that contracting delays but does not prevent the fall [CLM-003002, CLM-003015, CLM-003014, CLM-003084, CLM-003007]; J&J shows a diversified base absorbs a cliff [CLM-000254, CLM-002073].
3. **AI has no independent outcome yet in any of the four companies** (INTERPRETATION, status PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE): the registry records announcements, partnerships and devices, but no cost, timeline or revenue change attributable to an AI programme [CLM-011052, CLM-006054, CLM-006007]. The film therefore says "no independent evidence found", not "none exists".
4. **Takeda's evidenced strengths are the compounding base** (FACT): >270 plasma centres and fractionation expansion [CLM-005063, CLM-005065, CLM-005060], in-house orexin science discovered in Shonan with oveporexton approved August 2026 [CLM-005048, CLM-005029], rusfertide approved and zasocitinib under priority review [CLM-005032, CLM-005035], three positive Phase 3 readouts on plan [CLM-005042], and demonstrated stop discipline (cell-therapy exit, JPY 58.2bn impairment) [CLM-009084].
5. **The strategy v2 bets are gated recommendations with an explicit envelope** (RECOMMENDATION + ASSUMPTION): about USD 1.92bn committed at gates over 2027–2040 plus about USD 1.03bn contingent for licences (ASM-002, ASM-118), roughly 3% of fourteen years of R&D at today's level. The film labels this **ASSUMPTION · NOT A TAKEDA BUDGET** on screen and in narration.
6. **The independent council found no funded bet robust to severe pricing pressure** (council record) — which is the reason the gates exist, and the film says so rather than hiding it.

## What the film must never say

- That Takeda can eliminate LOE or "prevent" Entyvio erosion (the plan is "delay and measure").
- That the envelope is an approved budget, or that any figure in the strategy is Takeda's own plan.
- That AI, automation or any platform has produced a demonstrated outcome at these companies.
- That a large acquisition is impossible — only that a USD 5bn purchase fails the leverage arithmetic as framed (BET-15 rejected; 2.6x adjusted net debt/EBITDA with the Amitiza provision pending [CLM-005005, CLM-005014]).

## Emotional arc (narrative shape, not content)

Recognition (S01) → Tension (S02–S05) → Discovery (S06) → Strategic possibility (S07–S10) → Credible action (S11–S13) → Conviction (S14). Competitors occupy S02–S04 only (19% of narration); every later scene is Takeda-centred.
