# Narrative priorities — what made the cut and why

The MEDICINE 2040 encyclopedia holds 1,401 claims across 524 sources, eleven strategy pillars and 18 bets. A 6½-minute film can carry about 950 words. These are the selection rules that were applied, in order, and what they excluded.

## Selection rules

1. **Takeda first.** The film opens and closes on Takeda. Competitors appear only in S02–S04 (≈75 s, 19% of narration) and only as mechanisms that transfer to Takeda's situation. Target was 10–20%; achieved 19.2%.
2. **One mechanism per competitor.** Lilly = concentration risk before LOE. AbbVie = successor-before-cliff and the limits of contracting. J&J = diversification plus the cross-company AI reality check. Everything else about these companies (pipelines, deals, manufacturing capex) stayed in the encyclopedia.
3. **Only SUPPORTED or clearly labelled claims.** 70 of 82 on-screen claim references are SUPPORTED; 10 are PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED and 2 INCONCLUSIVE, and each of those is framed as an absence of evidence ("no independent evidence found", "none signed has produced an approved product", "savings unpublished") or carries its qualifier in the on-screen kind label.
4. **Numbers only where the registry has the number.** Every figure spoken is a registry value with its period (FY2025 Entyvio JPY 958.0bn; Humira USD 21.2bn → 4.5bn; Stelara −41.3%; Cerevel impairment USD 4.5bn Q4-2024; Nimbus USD 4.0bn upfront; leverage 2.6x; 63% GenAI adoption; 170 Lessines agents; 270+ plasma centres).
5. **Kinds preserved on screen.** SCENARIO (erosion band), ASSUMPTION (envelope; ASM-002/ASM-118; ASM-033 licence channel), RECOMMENDATION (BET ids), INTERPRETATION (data-moat, analogies) are stamped as chips or eyebrow labels, not blended into facts.
6. **Strategy v2 over v1.** Where the council changed a bet (BET-15 large acquisition rejected as framed; BET-18 licensing channel added; biosimilar playbook moved to 2028 H1), the film follows v2 and the Director Decision Record.

## Deliberately excluded

- Any valuation, share-price or market-cap commentary.
- Specific head-count or site-closure recommendations.
- Lilly/J&J/AbbVie manufacturing capex figures (true, but not transferable at Takeda's scale).
- The full 18-bet list; the film names only the bets that appear on screen (BET-01, 02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 09, 10, 11, 12, 15, 17, 18).
- Hypothetical investment amounts beyond the envelope figure, which appears once with its ASSUMPTION label.
