# Scientific review — is the film saying anything the science does not support?

Scope: pharmacology, regulatory and clinical statements in narration and on-screen text, reviewed against the registry statements and the strategy's own caveats. Reviewer stance: adversarial.

## Statements checked

| Statement | Assessment |
|---|---|
| "Entyvio… its exclusivity ends in the second half of this decade" (S01) | Takeda's own 20-F wording (CLM-005017). Exact biosimilar timing is uncertain; patents on formulation/dosing/process run to 2032. The on-screen markers show the dated events separately (Medicare 2028; FDA review ongoing; 2032 patents). Acceptable. |
| "two biosimilar applications are already under FDA review" (S05) | AVT16 (Alvotech) and PB016 (Fresenius Kabi/Polpharma) acceptance is from trade reporting and company releases (PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED). Not an approval claim; decision dates not stated as facts. Acceptable with the qualifier. |
| "If Entyvio erodes the way Humira did" (S05) | Analogy, explicitly a SCENARIO. Mechanistic differences (vedolizumab IV→SC mix, gut-selective positioning, Medicare negotiation preceding biosimilar entry) are not in the film; the strategy document carries them. The band overstates the first three years (U.S. event on a global base) — stated in `accuracy_notes` and in the strategy. Acceptable because labelled "not a forecast". |
| "Its Shonan labs discovered oveporexton, approved in August twenty twenty-six for narcolepsy" (S06) | FDA approval 5 Aug 2026 for narcolepsy type 1 in adults (CLM-005029). Narration says "narcolepsy" (word budget); the on-screen card reads "oveporexton approved (narcolepsy type 1)". Acceptable; any clinical re-use should keep the subtype in the voice track too. |
| "Rusfertide followed; zasocitinib is under priority review" (S06) | Rusfertide approved 28 Aug 2026 (polycythemia vera); zasocitinib NDA accepted under Priority Review 14 Sep 2026 (CLM-005032, CLM-005035). Correct as of the research cutoff (2026-11-21). Status may change; film states dates. |
| "Three Phase three readouts came in positive, on plan" (S06) | Dec 2024 plan named oveporexton, zasocitinib, rusfertide; all three read out positive (CLM-005042). Correct. |
| "Takeda has shown it can stop: it exited cell therapy and took the write-down" (S06) | JPY 58.2bn gamma-delta T-cell impairment (CLM-009084). Correct. |
| "An AI that plans its own experiments is a prototype; first clinical data… a year or two away. A fully autonomous discovery loop has not been demonstrated anywhere" (S09) | "Anywhere" is bounded by the sources opened (INCONCLUSIVE claims). The on-screen ladder says "not demonstrated" without "anywhere". Recommendation: read "anywhere" as "in the sources reviewed"; documented here and in `editorial_review.md` residual risks. |
| "Pharmacy benefit managers now decide biosimilar outcomes" (S10) | Strong generalisation from the CVS–Stelara template move and the Humira contracting history. INTERPRETATION; supported by CLM-007015 and CLM-003007. Acceptable in a film register; the strategy document uses "increasingly decide". |
| "The subcutaneous pen drives uptake and the HYHUB device brings plasma therapy home" (S10) | Takeda 20-F statements (CLM-007041, CLM-007042). Correct; "drives uptake" is Takeda's own description. |
| "No company studied publishes adherence outcomes" (S10) | Bounded to the four companies studied; INCONCLUSIVE claim worded as absence (CLM-007039). Correct. |
| "AbbVie wrote off half of Cerevel within months" (S11) | USD 4.5bn emraclidine impairment after Nov 2024 EMPOWER misses on an USD 8.7bn purchase closed Aug 2024 (CLM-009053, CLM-009054). "Half" = 52%. Correct. |
| "Regeneron's discovery platform did not stop its lead product falling forty-two percent" (S12) | EYLEA U.S. net sales −42% in 2025 (CLM-011017). "Lead product" refers to U.S. sales of the franchise; competition and the shift to EYLEA HD are the stated causes. Acceptable. |
| "plasma process data, and orexin biology" as Takeda's own compounding strengths (S12) | Plasma network (CLM-005063) and orexin franchise discovered in-house (CLM-005048). INTERPRETATION consistent with strategy v2 (BET-02, BET-12). |
| "The Medicare price lands" in 2028 (S13) | CMS negotiated price effective 1 Jan 2028 (CLM-007026). Correct. |

## Verdict
No statement contradicts the registry. Four statements are generalisations or analogies and are labelled as such on screen (SCENARIO / INTERPRETATION) or bounded by "the research found". Two precision notes for future cuts: say "narcolepsy type 1" in the narration as the card already does; soften "anywhere" to "in the sources reviewed".
