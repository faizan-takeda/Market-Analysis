## 2. How to register evidence

Shard files are plain JSONL: `New_mediciine/evidence_library/shards/<shard>/sources.jsonl` and `claims.jsonl`.
You may append lines directly with a script, or use the CLI (validates as it writes):

```bash
PY=~/.local/share/mise/installs/python/3.12/bin/python3
cd ~/Desktop/MEDICINE
$PY tools/evidence.py ids A1_lilly                  # shows next free IDs
$PY tools/evidence.py fetch "https://..." --grep "tirzepatide" --store-raw   # retrieves, hashes, extracts text, greps
$PY tools/evidence.py add-source --shard A1_lilly --json /tmp/src.json
$PY tools/evidence.py add-claim  --shard A1_lilly --json /tmp/clm.json
$PY tools/evidence.py validate --shard A1_lilly --path New_mediciine/evidence_library/shards/A1_lilly/sources.jsonl
```

```json
{"source_id":"SRC-001001"*, "title":"Eli Lilly Q4 2025 earnings release (8-K Ex. 99.1)"*,
 "publisher":"Eli Lilly and Company via SEC EDGAR"*, "company":"eli_lilly"*,
 "domain":"financials"*, "source_type":"earnings_release"*,
 "original_url":"https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/59478/..."*, "canonical_url":"..."*,
 "publication_date":"2026-02-05", "reporting_period":"FY2025", "access_date":"2026-11-21"*,
 "credibility":"T1_primary_official"*, "access_status":"retrieved"*,
 "document_identifier":"0000059478-26-000xxx", "locator":"Table 'Revenue by product', p.7",
 "inspected_passage":"<= 600 chars verbatim from the document, exactly as it appears>",
 "sha256":"optional from fetch", "raw_path":"optional", "extracted_text_path":"optional",
 "licensing":"public record | company IR (fair-use excerpt) | paywalled (metadata only)",
 "notes":"limitations, e.g. interested party; PDF page numbering offset"}
```

Enumerations: company `eli_lilly|johnson_johnson|abbvie|takeda|industry|other`; domain `business|financials|pipeline|scientific_rd|data_ai|robotics_automation|commercial_market_access|patient_care|partnerships_ma|policy_regulation|leadership|successes_failures|competitive_advantage|methodology|strategy`; source_type `sec_filing|annual_report|earnings_release|earnings_transcript|investor_presentation|press_release|company_web|regulatory_record|clinical_trial_registry|peer_reviewed|preprint|patent|lobbying_disclosure|government_record|news_tier1|news_trade|industry_report|academic_report|interview_transcript|podcast_video|dataset_api|other`; credibility `T1_primary_official|T2_company_technical|T3_independent_reputable|T4_expert_commentary|T5_speculative`; access_status `retrieved|api_json|metadata_only|blocked|paywalled|unavailable`.

Rule: `access_status: retrieved` REQUIRES `inspected_passage`. If you could not open it, use `metadata_only` and say so in notes. Never paste a passage you did not see.

### Claim record

```json
{"claim_id":"CLM-001001"*, "statement":"<= 600 chars, one factual sentence with date/period>"*,
 "kind":"FACT|INTERPRETATION|HYPOTHESIS|SCENARIO|RECOMMENDATION"*,
 "status":"SUPPORTED|PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED|CONTRADICTED|INCONCLUSIVE|NEEDS_MORE_RESEARCH"*,
 "companies":["eli_lilly"]*, "domain":"data_ai"*, "supporting_source_ids":["SRC-001001"]*,
 "locator":"section/table/page/timestamp inside the source", "dates":{"event":"2025-09-16","period":"FY2025"},
 "contrary_evidence":"text or null", "contrary_source_ids":[],
 "confidence_rationale":"why we believe it: who said it, interested party?, corroborated?"*,
 "single_interested_source":true, "last_verified":"2026-11-21"*,
 "maturity":"concept|prototype|pilot|operational|scaled|demonstrated_outcome|not_applicable|unknown",
 "entities":{"products":["tirzepatide"],"partners":["NVIDIA"],"technologies":["foundation model"]},
 "implication":"analyst interpretation, clearly separated from the fact",
 "related_reports":["New_mediciine/companies/eli_lilly/data_ai/ai_initiatives.md"], "tags":[]}
```

Rules: one statement per claim; FACT claims cite only sources you opened; the `implication` field is the only place for your interpretation; a company statement about intent is a FACT *that the statement was made*, not a FACT about outcome; every AI/robotics claim carries `maturity`.

## 3. Minimum deliverables per research agent

1. Markdown reports in your allowed directory (one per sub-domain) — every material sentence cites `[CLM-xxxxxx]`.
2. `map.md` inside your company/domain directory listing every file you wrote, one line each with relative link.
3. `evidence_gaps.md`: what you looked for and could not find/open; blocked URLs; unresolved questions.
4. `timeline.md` (company agents): dated events 2021-11 to 2026-11 with claim IDs.
5. Shard JSONL files validated with `evidence.py validate --shard <shard>` (0 errors) before you finish.
6. A completion report (your final message) in this exact shape:

```
FILES WRITTEN: <relative paths, one per line>
SOURCES: <n> (retrieved <n>, metadata_only <n>, blocked <n>)   CLAIMS: <n> (FACT <n>, other <n>)
VALIDATION: evidence.py validate output (0 errors required)
TOP 10 FINDINGS: <one line each, with claim IDs>
EVIDENCE GAPS: <bullets>
RECOMMENDED NEXT RESEARCH: <bullets>
```

## 4. Research conduct

- Source hierarchy T1 > T2 > T3 > T4; T5 never as fact. Press release = announcement happened, not value realized.
- Search by: company+domain, molecule/target, platform+partner, executive name, period, NCT number, deal identifier, DOI, and explicitly for *failure / delay / discontinued / criticism / lawsuit*.
- Open the primary document. Quote the passage. Record the locator.
- For every "capability" claim, ask: problem, what was built/bought, enablers (data/talent/IP/infra/partners), pilot vs production, operational change, measurable outcome, cost (if disclosed), defensibility.
- Seek contrary evidence for each major finding; record it in `contrary_evidence`.
- Rate-limit yourself (>= 1 s between requests to one host). Respect robots/terms. Do not bypass paywalls or logins. `investor.lilly.com` blocks scripted clients: use SEC EDGAR 8-K exhibits (`https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000059478&type=8-K`) and `lilly.com/news`.
- Bounded retries: 2 attempts per URL, then record `blocked|unavailable` and move on.
- Do not read the entire encyclopedia. Read `map.md` + your company/domain `map.md` + `tools/evidence.py ids <shard>` and begin.

## 5. Handoff hygiene

Save progressively (after every ~5 sources). Leave the shard valid at all times. If you run low on budget, write `evidence_gaps.md` and the completion report rather than another half-finished report.
