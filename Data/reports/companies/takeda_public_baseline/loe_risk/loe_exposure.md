# LOE exposure and revenue-replacement logic

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Takeda fiscal years run April-March (FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026). Claim IDs resolve in `New_mediciine/evidence_library/shards/A5_takeda/claims.jsonl`; `CLM-000xxx` IDs resolve in the canonical `claims_registry.jsonl`. Only public sources were used.

## Established
- **VYVANSE/ELVANSE:** JPY 423.2bn (FY2023) to 350.6bn to 203.2bn (FY2025). U.S. composition-of-matter patent and pediatric exclusivity expired August 2023; Takeda expects the decline to continue in FY2026 [CLM-005015].
- **AZILVA** fell to JPY 7.1bn after a February 2023 Japanese generic approval. **TRINTELLIX** (JPY 121.8bn) faces generics after certain patent rights expire in December 2026 [CLM-005016].
- **ENTYVIO** (JPY 958.0bn, 21% of revenue): regulatory exclusivity ends in the latter half of the decade; some patents expire in 2032; biosimilar timing is uncertain [CLM-005017]. Disclosed defences: subcutaneous Pen and pediatric labels [CLM-005018]. H1 FY2026 ENTYVIO growth is +2.7% at constant rates [CLM-005012].
- **TAKHZYRO** U.S. patent is listed to January 2036; no U.S. generic of GATTEX had launched by March 2026 [CLM-005019].
- **Leuprorelin:** the Hikari supply disruption produced a USD 505m award to AbbVie [CLM-005020].
- Velcade history was not examined *(gap)*.

## Replacement plan as stated by Takeda
Growth & Launch Products were 51% of FY2025 revenue [CLM-005008]. Takeda says six late-stage programs could reach USD 10-20bn combined peak revenue [CLM-005041] (a company statement, T2: not adjusted for probability of success and "not a forecast or target" [CLM-014049]), and the CEO cited three launches within 12 months of June 2026 [CLM-005074]. The estimate is a company statement; its basis footnote was not captured.

## Interpretation
*(INTERPRETATION)* Vyvanse removed about JPY 220bn of annual revenue in two years. Trintellix and Entyvio together are about 24% of FY2025 revenue. The launch wave starts from a base still shrinking at constant currency (H1 FY2026 revenue -1.0% CER) [CLM-005011].

## Contested / unknown
Entyvio erosion after 2030-32 depends on biosimilar timing and litigation, which are unknown: some patents expire in 2032 with UK and Netherlands revocation actions filed February-April 2026, and two biosimilar BLAs (Alvotech AVT16, Fresenius/Polpharma PB016) are accepted for FDA review, exclusivity status unconfirmed [CLM-014050] [CLM-014051]. Entyvio was selected for Medicare negotiation from January 2028 [CLM-007026] [CLM-008022]. Internal erosion curves are on the backlog (IVB-004).
