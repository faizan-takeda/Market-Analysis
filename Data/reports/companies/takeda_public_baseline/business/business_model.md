# Takeda business model and financial architecture

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Takeda fiscal years run April-March (FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026). Amounts are IFRS, JPY billion unless stated. Annual XBRL series are in [../financials/xbrl_annual_facts.md](../financials/xbrl_annual_facts.md) and [../financials/edgar_annual_facts.md](../financials/edgar_annual_facts.md) (not modified). H1 FY2026 (April-September 2026) is a partial year.

## Established
- **Scale and trend.** Revenue FY2021-FY2025: 3,569.0; 4,027.5; 4,263.8; 4,581.6; 4,505.7 [CLM-005000]. FY2025 fell 1.7% at actual exchange rates (2.7% at constant rates); U.S. revenue fell 9.0% to JPY 2,164.8bn [CLM-005001]. Mix FY2025: U.S. about 48%, Japan about 10%, China about 4% (arithmetic on reported figures) [CLM-005002] [CLM-005071].
- **Profit.** Core Operating Profit (company non-IFRS): about 955.2 (FY2021, implied), 1,188.4, 1,054.9, 1,162.6, 1,172.5 [CLM-005003]. FY2021 profit attributable to owners was JPY 230.2bn and operating cash flow JPY 1,123.1bn [CLM-000219] [CLM-000227]. Reported FY2025 operating profit was JPY 6.2bn and net loss JPY 152.1bn after a JPY 403.5bn legal provision for the AMITIZA verdict; Core Operating Profit was unaffected [CLM-005014].
- **Reinvestment.** R&D expense 526.1; 633.3; 729.9; 730.2; 675.9 (FY2021-25) [CLM-005004]. Capex 496.7; 319.4; 426.9 (FY2023-25) [CLM-005007].
- **Product concentration.** ENTYVIO JPY 958.0bn (21%), immunoglobulins 790.6bn (18%), TAKHZYRO 223.9bn (5%) [CLM-005009]. Growth & Launch Products were 43%, 48% and 51% of revenue in FY2023-FY2025 [CLM-005008] [CLM-007027].
- **Plasma-derived therapies.** Collection network: 233 centres (FY2022), 260 (FY2023), 275 (FY2024) [CLM-005063]; USD 230m Los Angeles expansion [CLM-005065]; 99.6% on-time in-full order release in FY2025 (company report) [CLM-014046].
- **Capital allocation.** Dividend JPY 200 a share in FY2025 (JPY 204 intended for FY2026), dividends paid JPY 313.2bn [CLM-005006]. Adjusted net debt to adjusted EBITDA: 2.6x (March 2023), 3.1x, 2.8x, 2.6x (March 2026) [CLM-005005] (confirmed in the 20-F text; see [../master_report.md](../master_report.md) §1). Takeda reports no significant acquisitions of businesses or assets in the three years to March 2026 [CLM-009087]; business development is licensing and upfronts [CLM-014060] [CLM-014061] [CLM-014062].
- **Write-downs.** ALOFISEL JPY 74.0bn and EXKIVITY JPY 28.5bn (FY2023) [CLM-012122]; Maverick JPY 27.8bn and soticlestat JPY 21.5bn (FY2024) [CLM-009083]; gamma delta T-cell platform JPY 58.2bn and ALUNBRIG JPY 31.9bn (FY2025) [CLM-009084] [CLM-012123]; Teva JV dissolution JPY 18.9bn [CLM-009085].
- **Cost programme.** May 2024 efficiency programme [CLM-005077]; March 2026 transformation targeting over JPY 200bn annualised gross savings by FY2028 [CLM-005059]. Restructuring cost series: about JPY 150bn (25 March 2026) → 170.0bn (original FY2026 forecast, May 2026) → 160.0bn (revised, October 2026) [CLM-005078] [CLM-005011].
- **H1 FY2026 (partial year).** Revenue JPY 2,407.0bn (+8.4% actual rates, -1.0% constant rates); Core Operating Profit guidance JPY 1,190bn [CLM-005011]; ENTYVIO +2.7% at constant rates [CLM-005012].

## Contested
- Core measures exclude items such as the AMITIZA provision, so reported and core profit diverge in FY2025; Takeda disputes the verdict [CLM-005013]. The final judgment amount and post-trial status are unverified [CLM-014048] [CLM-014047].
- The USD 10-20bn combined peak range for six late-stage programmes is a company statement, not adjusted for probability of success and "not a forecast or target" [CLM-005041] [CLM-014049] *(INTERPRETATION)* so it cannot be used to size replacement of the revenue at risk in [../loe_risk/loe_exposure.md](../loe_risk/loe_exposure.md).
