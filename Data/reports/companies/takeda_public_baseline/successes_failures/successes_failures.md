# Takeda: successes and failures, November 2021 to November 2026

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026. Outcomes are listed as disclosed; Takeda's efficacy statements are company-reported unless a regulator or peer-reviewed source is cited. Sentences marked *(INTERPRETATION)* are the analyst's reading.

## Successes
1. **Three novel FDA approvals in the window**: Fruzaqla 2023-11-08 [CLM-012018]; Orzeyful (oveporexton, narcolepsy type 1) 2026-08-05 [CLM-012019]; Mimrylo (rusfertide, polycythemia vera) 2026-08-28 [CLM-012020] [CLM-005029] [CLM-005032]. The zasocitinib NDA was accepted with Priority Review on 2026-09-14 [CLM-005035]; the R&D Day plan was met for the first three assets by November 2026 [CLM-005042].
2. **ENTYVIO subcutaneous conversion**: SC pen approved for UC (September 2023) and Crohn's disease (April 2024) and described as driving uptake [CLM-007033] [CLM-007041] [CLM-005018]; ENTYVIO JPY 958.0bn, 21% of revenue, +2.7% at constant rates in H1 FY2026 [CLM-007027] [CLM-005012].
3. **Growth & Launch Products reached 51% of revenue** in FY2025 (43% in FY2023) [CLM-005008]; QDENGA JPY 40.8bn [CLM-005045].
4. **Plasma network and operations**: 233 to 275 centres over FY2022-FY2024; 99.6% on-time in-full in FY2025 [CLM-005063] [CLM-014046].
5. **Portfolio discipline**: stopped programmes early (TAK-994, soticlestat, cell therapy) while Core Operating Profit stayed near JPY 1.05-1.19 trillion [CLM-005023] [CLM-005024] [CLM-005025] [CLM-005003] *(INTERPRETATION)*.

## Failures and weaknesses
1. **TAK-994**: Phase 2 stopped in 2021 for a liver-related safety signal and not developed further (decision June 2022); the Phase 3 pevonedistat MDS trial missed its event-free-survival endpoint [CLM-012119] [CLM-005023].
2. **EXKIVITY withdrawn globally** (October 2023) after EXCLAIM-2 missed its primary endpoint; JPY 28.5bn impairment [CLM-012121] [CLM-012122] [CLM-005022].
3. **ALOFISEL**: JPY 74.0bn impairment after the Phase 3 ADMIRE-CD II topline [CLM-012122].
4. **Soticlestat**: Phase 3 SKYLINE and SKYWAY missed primary endpoints (June 2024); no filing; JPY 21.5bn impairment; discontinued January 2025 [CLM-012120] [CLM-009083] [CLM-005024].
5. **Cell therapy exit**: all cell-therapy efforts discontinued on 2025-10-01; expected impairment JPY 58.0bn, recognised JPY 58.2bn on the gamma delta T-cell platform plus JPY 31.9bn on ALUNBRIG in the FY2025 20-F [CLM-005025] [CLM-009084] [CLM-012123].
6. **AMITIZA verdict**: jury verdict of 2026-05-18 for USD 884.9m single damages (subject to trebling for two groups); JPY 403.5bn provision; reported FY2025 operating profit JPY 6.2bn; Takeda intends post-trial motions and an appeal; final judgment amount and post-trial status unverified [CLM-005013] [CLM-005014] [CLM-014048] [CLM-014047].
7. **VYVANSE loss of exclusivity**: JPY 423.2bn (FY2023) to 203.2bn (FY2025); TRINTELLIX generics expected after December 2026 [CLM-005015] [CLM-005016].
8. **Partnership terminations**: Denali collaboration ended April 2026; Neurocrine terminated TAK-041 and TAK-831 (March 2025); TAK-004 discontinued (single secondary source) [CLM-009089] [CLM-009090] [CLM-005026].
9. **Manufacturing quality**: the 2020 Hikari warning letter led to a leuprorelin shortage and a USD 505m award to AbbVie (Delaware court, December 2023) [CLM-005020].
10. **Teva Takeda Pharma JV dissolved** with a JPY 18.9bn impairment [CLM-009085].
