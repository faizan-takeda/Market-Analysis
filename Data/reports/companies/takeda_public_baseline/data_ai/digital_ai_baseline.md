## Established
Takeda has a named C-level owner, a funded DD&T function, an insourcing decision, a governed agent platform and AI-focused research alliances.

## Contested
Depth of production use is described only by Takeda and a vendor; the BCG interview and Databricks page are interested sources. Fortune and Informatica leads were not verified [CLM-005062].

## Unknown *(gap)*
Number of AI systems in production, value or time saved, share of R&D decisions informed by models, lab automation, and whether the AIR PDF holds quantified results [CLM-005081, CLM-005080].
