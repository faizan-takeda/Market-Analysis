# Successes and failures

## Top successes
1. DARZALEX USD 14,351m in FY2025 from 6,023m in FY2021 [CLM-002023]; myeloma brands total USD 17.4bn [CLM-002072].
2. Absorbing the Stelara loss: group sales +6.0% in FY2025 with Stelara -41.3% [CLM-000254] [CLM-002075].
3. CARVYKTI USD 1,887m (+95.9%) [CLM-000257] and bispecifics TECVAYLI 670 / TALVEY 463 [CLM-002024].
4. Kenvue separation executed in two steps in 2023 with a USD 21.0bn gain [CLM-002006] [CLM-002007].
5. Shockwave and Abiomed integration: Shockwave 1,146m in FY2025; Abiomed 1,751m (+17.1%) [CLM-002011] [CLM-002027]; ICOTYDE approval [CLM-002065].
6. Intra-Cellular: CAPLYTA MDD approved in Q4 2025, nine months after the USD 14.5bn close; Momenta delivered IMAAVY (2025-04-30) [CLM-009030] [CLM-009031] [CLM-009044].
7. Regulatory firsts in devices: OTTAVA De Novo [CLM-014022]; MONARCH and VELYS 510(k) clearances [CLM-014025] [CLM-014026].

## Failures and weaknesses
1. Talc: three bankruptcy attempts, two dismissed (LTL 2021, 2023), Red River not confirmed 2025-03-31; USD ~7.0bn charge in FY2023 and ~5.0bn in 2024 [CLM-002015] [CLM-002016] [CLM-002017] [CLM-002043]; 2026 proposed USD 5.5bn+ resolution [CLM-002062] [CLM-002063].
2. Aticaprant Phase 3 stopped for insufficient efficacy [CLM-002039] [CLM-002056].
3. RSV adult vaccine and infectious-disease exits [CLM-002018]; COVID-19 vaccine exit costs USD 1.5bn [CLM-002019].
4. Bermekimab write-off ~USD 0.8bn [CLM-002020].
5. Varipulse U.S. pause: 4 of 132 patients with stroke/TIA vs ≤1% expected [CLM-002044] [CLM-002045]; PFA competition [CLM-002028].
6. Impella Class I recalls: 66,390 pumps, 129 serious injuries incl. 49 deaths (IFU correction); controller purge-retainer recall 2025 [CLM-002046] [CLM-002047].
7. Stelara cliff and Orthopaedics at 1.1% growth [CLM-002022] [CLM-002029]; IRA price negotiation [CLM-002054].
8. Varipulse: FDA correction notice records 4 of 132 (about 3%) stroke/TIA in the U.S. External Evaluation versus about 1% or less expected; Instructions for Use updated 2025-02-14 for all lots (correction, not removal) [CLM-014018].
9. Talc: Red River dismissed 2025-03-31, about USD 7bn of reserve reversed, USD 3.4bn reserve at Q4 2025; 2026-07-27 conditional USD 5.5bn resolution of about 76,000 claims needs at least 95% participation, outcome unknown [CLM-014027] [CLM-014028] [CLM-014029].
10. Acquisition write-downs: OTTAVA/Auris USD 0.9bn (FY2021), M710 about USD 0.2bn in each of 2023 and 2024, Laminar pivotal study suspended 2025, NM26 early-termination interim [CLM-009047] [CLM-009045] [CLM-009039] [CLM-009037]. Wave-2 corrections: aticaprant discontinuation date and cause confirmed (company release only) [CLM-014019].
11. OTTAVA: De Novo authorised but one announced installation site only; no adoption evidence [CLM-014022] [CLM-000284].

## Contested
