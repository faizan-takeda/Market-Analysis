# Commercial and market access

## Established
- **Stelara cliff.** Sales 9,134 (FY2021) → 10,858 (FY2023 peak) → 10,361 (FY2024) → 6,078 (FY2025, -41.3%); U.S. 6.7bn → 3.8bn [CLM-002022]. First U.S. biosimilars: Wezlana January 2025, then Pyzchiva, Yesintek, Selarsdi by late February [CLM-002052].
- **Successors.** TREMFYA 5,155 (+40.5%) covering about one third of Stelara's USD 4.3bn loss [CLM-002026]; Crohn's approval March 2025 [CLM-002053]; ICOTYDE approved 2026 [CLM-002065]. Immunology segment fell 11.8% [CLM-002026]. Q3 2026: Stelara ~690 bp drag [CLM-002060].
- **Medicare negotiation.** XARELTO, STELARA and IMBRUVICA were selected; Janssen sued in July 2023 [CLM-002021]; negotiated prices effective 2026-01-01, per-drug figures not inspected [CLM-002054]. Medicare Part D redesign cited for Invega and Stelara declines [CLM-002025].
- **340B.** J&J's contract-pharmacy delivery policy [CLM-002034]; 2025-06-27 court ruling that HRSA could require approval of the rebate model [CLM-002069]; HRSA's own rebate pilot enjoined 2025-12-29 [CLM-002070].
- **Patient support.** Janssen CarePath becoming J&J withMe, free with no income requirement for its savings programme [CLM-002071] [CLM-007035].
- **Negotiated prices (2026).** Stelara USD 4,695 (-66% vs 2023 list), Xarelto USD 197 (-62%), Imbruvica USD 9,319 (-38%) [CLM-008008] [CLM-008009] [CLM-008010]; Stelara and Xarelto deselected from 2027 [CLM-008011]; Erleada selected for 2028 [CLM-008015]; Supreme Court denied certiorari to Janssen on 2026-05-18 [CLM-008024].
- **Payer events.** Optum Rx excluded Stelara from two main formularies on 2025-07-01 with nine biosimilars on the market (trade commentary, partially supported) [CLM-007016]; CVS Caremark prefers Pyzchiva and Yesintek from 2026-07-01 [CLM-007015]. U.S. Stelara about USD 3.8bn in FY2025 [CLM-007017]. Part D redesign cut Erleada, Invega, Opsumit and Uptravi sales [CLM-007018].
- **Launch curves.** Carvykti USD 1,887m vs 963m (capacity-limited); Spravato USD 1,696m vs 1,077m, restricted to certified settings [CLM-007029] [CLM-007030]. 340B: D.C. Circuit 2026-07-21 [CLM-008039]; Stelara and Xarelto outside the 2027 HRSA pilot cohort [CLM-008041].
- **Other.** ERISA class action over J&J's own prescription-drug benefit management (allegation) [CLM-007050]. Horizontals: [commercial_patient](../../../industry_transformation/commercial_patient/map.md), [policy](../../../industry_transformation/policy/map.md).
