## Interpretation
- MedTech earns about one third of the Innovative Medicine margin on about 36% of sales; this gap frames the Orthopaedics separation [CLM-002004].
- Ex-Stelara growth was about +12% in FY2025, of which about 1 point was acquired [CLM-002075].
