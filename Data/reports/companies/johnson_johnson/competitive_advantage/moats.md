# Moats (INTERPRETATION; mechanism and replication path)

1. **Myeloma franchise depth.** Mechanism: CD38 antibody, BCMA CAR-T, two bispecifics in one commercial organisation; USD 17.4bn in 2025 [CLM-002072]. Replication: multi-year clinical programmes plus cell-therapy manufacturing; erosion risk from DARZALEX concentration [CLM-002073]. Confidence: medium.
2. **Pharma + MedTech dual platform.** Mechanism claimed by J&J: cardiovascular leadership across Abiomed, Biosense Webster and Shockwave [CLM-002074]. No disclosed cross-segment revenue synergy; the margin gap and the Orthopaedics separation point the other way [CLM-002004] [CLM-002037]. Confidence: low (HYPOTHESIS).
3. **Immunology scale and successor sequencing.** Mechanism: TREMFYA and ICOTYDE as IL-23 successors with Crohn's/UC trials [CLM-002055] [CLM-002053]. Replication: requires an IL-23 asset launched 3–5 years pre-LOE. Confidence: medium; Immunology still fell 11.8% [CLM-002026].
4. **M&A bridge capacity.** USD 17.5bn in 2025 alone, funded by debt [CLM-002013] [CLM-002014]; replicable by any well-capitalised peer.
5. **JLABS/JJDC ecosystem.** Contracting rather than expanding: JLABS planned at six sites with capacity for 225 companies in 2016 [CLM-009051]; Toronto, Houston and Washington D.C. exited, 11 sites in August 2025 [CLM-014064]; New York closure is a snippet [CLM-014065]. J&J claims more than 150 partnerships [CLM-009050]; no JJDC portfolio count or fund size found [CLM-014066]. Not evidenced as a moat *(gap)*.
6. **Surgical robotics option.** FDA records exist for OTTAVA (De Novo), MONARCH and VELYS [CLM-014022] [CLM-014025] [CLM-014026], but no installed base or procedure volume; OTTAVA has one announced installation site [CLM-000284]. Confidence: low *(INTERPRETATION)*.
