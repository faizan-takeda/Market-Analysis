## 1. Executive thesis
Lilly grew from USD 28.3bn to 65.2bn revenue (FY2021-25) [CLM-000133] [CLM-000137] on tirzepatide, which reached 56% of revenue [CLM-000242] [CLM-001003]. Nine-month 2026 revenue was USD 67.2bn [CLM-001032]. The USD 91-92bn 2026 revenue guidance was raised in the Q3 2026 release of 2026-10-29 from USD 85-87bn; the initial February 2026 guidance was USD 80-83bn [CLM-014001] [CLM-014002]. It financed this with margin expansion [CLM-001005], debt [CLM-000202] and USD 21bn+ Indiana plus >USD 50bn U.S. site commitments [CLM-001039] [CLM-001041]. Whether it is positioned beyond incretins remains *(HYPOTHESIS)*: orforglipron is approved [CLM-001030], retatrutide has positive Phase 3 [CLM-001060].
