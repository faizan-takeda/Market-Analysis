## Established / contested / unknown
- Eli Lilly reported spending $7,890,000 on U.S. federal lobbying in 2024 in its own political-contributions report. [CLM-008000] *Contrary/caveat:* A shareholder-proponent summary cites $8,430,000 for Lilly 2024 federal lobbying; the difference is unresolved.
- A shareholder-proponent summary hosted on the UN PRI platform states Lilly spent $8,430,000 on federal lobbying for 2024. [CLM-008001]
- The FEC lists Eli Lilly and Company Political Action Committee (C00082792) as a qualified PAC with Lobbyist/Registrant designation and a last filing date of 2026-10-19. [CLM-008002]
- The FEC lists Johnson & Johnson Political Action Committee (C00010983) as a qualified PAC active in cycles 2022, 2024 and 2026. [CLM-008003]
- The FEC lists AbbVie Political Action Committee (C00536573) as a qualified PAC active in cycles 2022, 2024 and 2026. [CLM-008004]
- An OpenSecrets page snippet for the Lilly PAC (C00082792), 2024 cycle, shows total spent of $2,194,731. [CLM-008005]
- Johnson & Johnson disclosed that it paid PhRMA more than $500,000 in 2023 dues, of which PhRMA reports about 8% was used for federal lobbying; BIO, NAM, AdvaMed and the U.S. Chamber are in the same tier. [CLM-008006]
- Lobbying and PAC records document activity and amounts only; this project draws no causal inference between lobbying or giving and any policy outcome. [CLM-008007] *(INTERPRETATION)*

**Unknown:** LDA totals and issues for all years, PAC receipts/disbursements totals (OpenFEC rate-limited), AbbVie trade-association list (blocked), PhRMA board roles (not verified).
