# Pricing actions, tariffs and manufacturing pledges

Scope: Lilly, J&J, AbbVie (Takeda briefly). Neutral: documents activity and outcomes only; no causal inference about influence. Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Sources: shard A8_policy.

| Company | Pledge (company statement) | Date | Claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lilly | >$50bn total U.S. commitments since 2020 ($23bn prior); the $27bn figure is derived | 2025-02-26 | [CLM-008029] |
| J&J | >$55bn over four years | 2025-03-21 | [CLM-008030] |
| AbbVie | $100bn over a decade (not $10bn) | 2026-01-12 | [CLM-008032] |
| Takeda | none found in 20-F text searched | n/a | [CLM-008035] |

- Lilly announced on March 1, 2023 a 70% price reduction on its most commonly prescribed insulins and an expansion of its program capping patient out-of-pocket cost at $35 or less per month. [CLM-008026]
- In a November 6, 2025 release Lilly stated that Medicare beneficiaries would pay no more than $50 per month for Zepbound and orforglipron (if approved) from as early as April 1, 2026, with three years of tariff relief and no future pricing mandates. [CLM-008027] *Contrary/caveat:* Lilly's 10-K says Medicare beneficiaries should have access by July 1, 2026.
- Lilly's FY2025 10-K says its preliminary agreements include Medicaid and other price measures, launch pricing aligned across developed nations, and a three-year Section 232 tariff grace period conditional on U.S. manufacturing commitments; definitive agreements were still being negotiated. [CLM-008028]
- Lilly said on February 26, 2025 that four new U.S. manufacturing sites bring total U.S. capital commitments to more than $50 billion since 2020, versus $23 billion for 2020-2024. [CLM-008029]
- Johnson & Johnson announced on March 21, 2025 more than $55 billion of U.S. manufacturing, R&D and technology investment over four years, a 25% increase on the prior four years. [CLM-008030]
- On January 8, 2026 Johnson & Johnson announced a voluntary agreement with the administration covering TrumpRx participation, Medicaid prices comparable to other developed countries and an exemption from tariffs, and reaffirmed its $55 billion investment through early 2029. [CLM-008031]
- On January 12, 2026 AbbVie announced a three-year voluntary agreement with $100 billion of U.S. R&D and capital investment over a decade, low Medicaid prices, TrumpRx offerings and exemption from tariffs and future pricing mandates. [CLM-008032] *Contrary/caveat:* Working figure of USD 10bn in the project brief is not supported by either source.
- The Federal Register published on April 16, 2025 a Commerce notice requesting comments on a Section 232 national-security investigation of imports of pharmaceuticals and pharmaceutical ingredients. [CLM-008033]
- Lilly's FY2025 10-K states pharmaceuticals are exempt from certain tariffs but that the exemptions may end, and that Lilly may be unable to share tariff cost burdens. [CLM-008034]
- Takeda's 20-F states a May 2025 executive order encouraged Most Favored Nation pricing and that the administration has negotiated MFN-style agreements with several companies, which could lower Takeda's U.S. prices. [CLM-008035]
- Takeda's 20-F states annual NHI price revisions since April 2021 have led to more frequent downward revisions in Japan, and a surcharge on long-listed originator drugs applies from June 1, 2026. [CLM-008036]
- Across Lilly, J&J and AbbVie, each January 2026 or November 2025 agreement pairs stated price or access concessions with a stated three-year (Lilly, AbbVie) or unspecified-term tariff exemption. [CLM-008037] *(INTERPRETATION)*
