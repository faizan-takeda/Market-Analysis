Revenue exposure by product: use Part D gross cost figures in the claims below and the already-registered financial claims (CLM-000001..000238); product-level company revenue not re-registered here (gap).

- CMS set a negotiated 30-day price of $4,695 for Stelara (Janssen) for 2026, a 66% discount from the $13,836 2023 list price; 2023 Part D gross cost was $2,988,560,000. [CLM-008008]
- CMS set a negotiated 30-day price of $197 for Xarelto (Janssen) for 2026, a 62% discount from the $517 2023 list price; 2023 Part D gross cost was $6,309,766,000. [CLM-008009]
- CMS set a negotiated 30-day price of $9,319 for Imbruvica (Pharmacyclics) for 2026, a 38% discount from the $14,934 2023 list price; 2023 Part D gross cost was $2,371,858,000. [CLM-008010]
- CMS deselected Stelara and Xarelto from the negotiation program effective January 1, 2027, with determinations in October 2025. [CLM-008011]
- CMS set a negotiated 2027 30-day price of $136 for Linzess (AbbVie), a 75% discount from the $539 2024 list price; 2024 Part D gross cost was $1,982,587,000. [CLM-008012]
- CMS set a negotiated 2027 30-day price of $770 for Vraylar (AbbVie), a 44% discount from the $1,376 2024 list price; 2024 Part D gross cost was $1,136,814,000. [CLM-008013]
- CMS estimated the second-cycle negotiated prices would have saved about $12 billion (about 44%) of 2024 net Part D spending on the 15 drugs, excluding Coverage Gap Discount Program effects. [CLM-008014]
- On January 27, 2026 CMS selected 15 drugs for the third cycle (prices effective 2028), including Lilly's Trulicity ($4.90B Part B/D spend) and Verzenio ($1.43B), J&J's Erleada ($1.95B), AbbVie's Botox ($1.14B) and Takeda's Entyvio ($1.48B). [CLM-008015]
- CMS announced on March 13, 2026 that the manufacturers of all 15 third-cycle drugs, including Lilly, Janssen and AbbVie, chose to participate in negotiation. [CLM-008016]
- Third-cycle negotiated prices were not located in any source opened; negotiations were to end by November 1, 2026. [CLM-008017]
- AbbVie's FY2025 10-K states CMS selected Imbruvica (2023), Vraylar and Linzess (2025) and Botox (January 2026) for government-set Medicare prices. [CLM-008018]
- AbbVie recorded a pre-tax $2.1 billion impairment on the Imbruvica intangible in Q3 2023 after its IRA selection (fair value $1.9B versus carrying value $4.0B). [CLM-008019]
- AbbVie states that from January 2025 the Part D coverage gap discount was replaced by a 10% manufacturer discount (and 20% above the $2,000 out-of-pocket threshold). [CLM-008020]
- Lilly's FY2025 10-K reports Jardiance (Boehringer collaboration) in the first cycle at a 66% discount, Trulicity and Verzenio selected January 2026, and expects more Lilly products to be selected. [CLM-008021]
- Takeda's 20-F states CMS selected Entyvio, its highest-selling product in the year ended March 31, 2026, for negotiation effective January 2028. [CLM-008022]
- Janssen sued HHS/CMS in July 2023 challenging the negotiation program on First and Fifth Amendment grounds; the Third Circuit affirmed denial of its summary judgment motion and Janssen petitioned the Supreme Court in December 2025. [CLM-008023]
- The U.S. Supreme Court denied certiorari in Janssen Pharmaceuticals v. Kennedy (No. 25-749) on May 18, 2026, along with petitions from other manufacturers. [CLM-008024]
- J&J's FY2025 10-K reports U.S. Stelara sales of about $3.8 billion and Immunology sales down 11.8%, driven by Stelara biosimilars, with more biosimilar launches expected. [CLM-008025]
