# Litigation with a policy dimension

Scope: Lilly, J&J, AbbVie (Takeda briefly). Neutral: documents activity and outcomes only; no causal inference about influence. Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Sources: shard A8_policy.

## Litigation, 340B, compounding, patents
- Lilly sued HRSA in November 2024 over its rejection of a 340B cash replenishment model; the D.D.C. ruled in May 2025 that HRSA must preapprove such a model and Lilly appealed. [CLM-008038]
- On July 21, 2026 the D.C. Circuit held that manufacturers cannot adopt 340B rebate models without HRSA approval, in a consolidated appeal arising from suits that include J&J (filed 2024-11-12) and Lilly (filed 2024-11-14). [CLM-008039]
- HRSA's first 340B rebate pilot (announced July 2025) was blocked by a federal court and withdrawn in February 2026; a revised pilot notice was published August 3, 2026 for January 1, 2027, limited to drugs with negotiated prices. [CLM-008040]
- HRSA's 2027 rebate pilot cohort of 10 manufacturers and 21 drugs does not include J&J's Stelara and Xarelto, which left the negotiation program. [CLM-008041]
- Hospitals sued Lilly in July 2026 over its policy requiring 340B claims data for all covered drugs, according to HFMA. [CLM-008042]
- Lilly's 10-K says its January 2021 suit against HHS over 340B contract-pharmacy policy remains pending at the Seventh Circuit, and a Mosaic Health class action against Lilly and others is ongoing. [CLM-008043]
- J&J's 10-K describes a policy limiting contract-pharmacy 340B deliveries for non-grantee covered entities, with exceptions. [CLM-008044]
- FDA's December 19, 2024 declaratory order found the tirzepatide shortage resolved; tirzepatide was listed December 15, 2022 and delisted October 2, 2024. [CLM-008045]
- Lilly's 10-K says mass-compounded incretins persist and Lilly will consider lawsuits against unlawful practices. [CLM-008046]
- An April 20, 2026 N.D. Cal. order granted in part and denied in part a motion to dismiss in Lilly v. Mochi Health (No. 3:25-cv-03534), a case over compounded tirzepatide marketing. [CLM-008047]
- The Seventh Circuit affirmed dismissal of the Humira patent-thicket antitrust complaint (Baltimore v. AbbVie) on August 1, 2022. [CLM-008048]
- In January 2025 payors filed a putative class action alleging AbbVie's rebating practices impair Humira biosimilar competition. [CLM-008049]
- J&J's talc strategy moved from a prepackaged plan of about $6.475 billion present value (May 2024) to the Red River bankruptcy (September 2024), dismissed in March 2025; about $7 billion of reserve was reversed and about $3.4 billion remained at Q4 2025. [CLM-008050]
- J&J's July 2021 opioid settlement of state and subdivision claims was up to $5.0 billion, about 80% paid by end of FY2025; about 23 state cases and 285 MDL cases remain. [CLM-008051]
- Lilly faces insulin-pricing MDL litigation in D.N.J. and settled with New York (2023) and Minnesota (2024) without payment or admission. [CLM-008052] *Contrary/caveat:* Project brief dated the Minnesota settlement 2023.

- On January 26, 2024 Sen. Sanders announced J&J, Merck and BMS CEOs agreed to testify voluntarily, ending a planned subpoena vote. [CLM-008054]
