# External-innovation machinery

## Eli Lilly
Lilly Catalyze360 bundles Lilly Ventures (capital), Lilly Gateway Labs (lab space), Lilly ExploR&D (development expertise) and, since September 2025, TuneLab [CLM-009025]. TuneLab uses federated learning on Lilly models [CLM-009026]; Benchling plans distribution to 1,300+ customers [CLM-009027]; a vendor reports 2M Lilly and 675K partner data points by Oct 2026 [CLM-009028]. The NVIDIA lab adds up to $1B joint spend [CLM-009029]. Fund size and investment counts for Lilly Ventures were not found (gap).

## Johnson & Johnson
J&J Innovation operates regional Innovation Centers, JLABS and JJDC (venture arm) with 150+ claimed partnerships [CLM-009050]. JLABS was planned in 2016 at six sites with capacity for 225 companies [CLM-009051]; by March 2026 trade press reports closures of Houston, Toronto, Washington DC and New York [CLM-009052]. Resident-company counts and JJDC fund size are not in the sources opened (gap).

## AbbVie
AbbVie Ventures is the corporate venture group; sources conflict on founding (2009 vs about 2015) [CLM-009074] and one source is sponsored content [CLM-009075]. AbbVie's real machinery in the audited record is deal flow: yearly early-stage upfronts of $0.6-1.0B [CLM-009069], options (Calico, ADARx) [CLM-009068] [CLM-009061] and BaseLaunch membership [CLM-009094]. The brief's "external-innovation share of pipeline" figure was not found (gap).

## Takeda
Takeda's Center for External Innovation (head Alison Handley) sits on BaseLaunch's investment board [CLM-009091]; Takeda Ventures reportedly sits under it, per an aggregator only [CLM-009092]. The 20-F lists dozens of licence partners [CLM-009088]. Takeda Digital Ventures, T-CiRA and fund sizes were not verified (gap).
