# Launch execution evidence

## What is established
- **Mounjaro/Zepbound**: Mounjaro $22,965m (2024: $11,540m) and Zepbound $13,542m (2024: $4,926m); together 56% of Lilly revenue [CLM-007031] [CLM-001003]. Growth came with lower realized prices [CLM-007024]. Supply and channel moves: vials and self-pay [CLM-007000], retail pickup [CLM-007003], KwikPen [CLM-007044].
- **Skyrizi/Rinvoq**: Skyrizi $17,562m (+49.9%) and Rinvoq $8,304m (+39.1%); 10-K attributes growth to share uptake and market growth; Humira fell 49% [CLM-007020]. Humira defence tactics: [CLM-003085].
- **Carvykti**: $1,887m vs $963m, attributed to share gains and capacity expansion, so supply governed pace [CLM-007029].
- **Spravato**: $1,696m vs $1,077m; distribution restricted to certified healthcare settings, no home dispensing [CLM-007029] [CLM-007030].
- **Tremfya**: $5,155m vs $3,670m; Crohn's approval March 2025 and Stelara-successor framing [CLM-007029] [CLM-002053].
- **Kisunla**: $21m in Q1 2025 (snippet only, source blocked) and $484m in 9M 2026 [CLM-007032] [CLM-001032]. Reasons for slow uptake are not verified.
- **Entyvio pen (Takeda)**: US approvals Sept 2023 (UC) and April 2024 (CD); 20-F says SC drives uptake [CLM-007033] [CLM-007041]; call summary credits pen adoption and formulary wins [CLM-007043]. Planned launches: oveporexton, zasocitinib [CLM-007033].

## What is contested
- Whether Kisunla's pace reflects MRI/ARIA burden, diagnosis pathway or price: not established here [CLM-007032].
- How much of Skyrizi/Rinvoq growth is contracting versus clinical preference: 10-K gives only share language [CLM-007020].

## What is unknown
- Field-force sizes, omnichannel metrics and hub-service performance for any company (not found in sources opened).
- Qdenga launch curve beyond a +22.1% growth figure in an aggregator summary [CLM-007043].
