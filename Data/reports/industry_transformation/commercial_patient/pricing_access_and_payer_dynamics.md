# Pricing, access and payer dynamics

## What is established

### LillyDirect cash price history (Zepbound vials)
- Aug 2024: 2.5 mg $399, 5 mg $549 per four weeks [CLM-007000].
- 25 Feb 2025: 7.5 mg and 10 mg vials launched at $499 with the Self Pay Journey Program (snippet only) [CLM-007001].
- 1 Dec 2025: $299 / $399 / $449 (2.5 mg / 5 mg / 7.5-15 mg), down from $349 / $499 / $499; non-program prices $599-$1,049 [CLM-007002].
- Feb 2026: KwikPen at the same self-pay prices from $299 [CLM-007044].
- Nov 2025 US government agreement and TrumpRx linkage: [CLM-001036] [CLM-007007] [CLM-007008].

### Gross-to-net and rebates (as disclosed)
- AbbVie: rebate and chargeback provisions $65.1bn (2025), $59.3bn (2024), $56.8bn (2023) against 2025 net revenue of $61.2bn [CLM-007019].
- Lilly: 2025 revenue growth from volume "partially offset by lower realized prices", mostly Mounjaro and Zepbound [CLM-007024].
- Takeda: rebate details not public; formulary exclusion directly reduces usage [CLM-007025].
- J&J: no gross-to-net figure in the passages read; Stelara decline cut Innovative Medicine growth by about 10.4 points [CLM-007017].

- Lilly: Jardiance (2026), Trulicity and Verzenio (2028) [CLM-007023].
- Takeda: Entyvio (2028); Part D redesign expected to reduce profit; MFN executive order of May 2025 [CLM-007026].

## What is contested
- Whether the 2025 CVS decision reflected a better Wegovy net price (CVS says so [CLM-007011]) or leverage over access; the reversal after about 11 months [CLM-007013] does not settle it.
- Whether PBM private labels "pop" or exploit the gross-to-net bubble: Drug Channels argues both [CLM-007016] (commentary).

## What is unknown
- Net prices, rebate rates by drug, and the share of self-pay revenue.
- Whether the Oct 2026 CVS change increased Zepbound volume.
