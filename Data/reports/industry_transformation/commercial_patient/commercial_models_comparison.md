## What is contested

- Whether cash channels take volume from insured patients: copay accumulator rules and anti-kickback limits restrict use of discounts together with insurance [CLM-007010]. Lilly's "1 in 3 new starts" is a new-start measure with unverified footnotes [CLM-007004].
- Whether PBM exclusivity persists: CVS preferred Wegovy over Zepbound for 11 months, then reinstated Zepbound [CLM-007011] [CLM-007013].
- Whether TrumpRx volumes are material: CRS reports listings and manufacturer counts, not sales; agreements expire with the current term [CLM-007009].

## What is unknown

- Self-pay share of Zepbound revenue (no number in the 10-K [CLM-007005]).
- Net price per prescription and gross-to-net for any company except AbbVie's rebate provisions [CLM-007019].
- Any adherence or time-to-therapy outcome for any patient support program [CLM-007039].
