# Patient programs and care delivery

## Patient support and adherence
- Lilly: insulin list cut of 70% and $35 monthly cap announced 1 March 2023 (company-reported $21.80 average out-of-pocket) [CLM-007034]; LillyDirect >1 million patients in 2025 (company-reported) [CLM-007004].
- J&J: J&J withMe, free, no income requirement for its savings programme [CLM-007035] [CLM-002071].
- AbbVie: Allē >6 million members, 19,000 practices (Jan 2024) [CLM-007036]; October 2024 redesign reversed in early 2025 [CLM-003033].
- Takeda: Help at Hand >100,000 patients cumulative [CLM-007037]; EntyvioConnect described in search snippets only [CLM-007038].
- Outcome evidence (adherence, persistence, time-to-therapy): none found for any company; counts are enrolment or access numbers, company-reported [CLM-007039].

## Care-delivery innovation
- Self-administration and device shifts: Zepbound KwikPen and vials [CLM-007044]; Entyvio SC pen [CLM-007041]; Hyqvia HYHUB needle-free transfer, FDA 510(k) July 2025 (clearance, not adoption) [CLM-007042].
- Counter-trend: Spravato must be administered in certified settings under observation [CLM-007030].
- Connected devices: J&J reports strong Impella adoption but the passage describes no connectivity feature [CLM-007047]. Lilly connected pens (Tempo) not found in the 10-K text searched.
- Decentralized trials: no usable disclosure in the Lilly or J&J 10-Ks [CLM-007048].
- Digital therapeutics calibration: Pear Therapeutics filed Chapter 11 on 7 April 2023 despite published evidence, with the former CEO blaming payment denial [CLM-007045]; commentary says evidence was strong but reimbursement stalled growth [CLM-007046].

## What is contested
- Whether Pear's failure was reimbursement or product-market fit: sources conflict in emphasis [CLM-007045] [CLM-007046].
