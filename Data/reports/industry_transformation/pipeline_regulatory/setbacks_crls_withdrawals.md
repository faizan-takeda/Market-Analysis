## Pattern *(INTERPRETATION)*

- Neuroscience/psychiatry is the common failure site across three companies (J&J aticaprant, AbbVie emraclidine, Takeda soticlestat) [CLM-012135][CLM-012115][CLM-012120].
- Manufacturing and device inspection findings, not clinical data, caused the Lilly lebrikizumab CRL and AbbVie's second ABBV-951 CRL [CLM-012134][CLM-012112].
- Takeda carries the most accelerated-approval and cell-therapy attrition in the window [CLM-012121][CLM-012122][CLM-012123].
