# Pipeline quality and revenue-replacement assessment (INTERPRETATION)

Everything below is analyst interpretation of the cited FACT claims, not a finding of fact.

## Depth

- Lilly: eight CDER novel approvals plus the Zepbound NDA, and eight Phase 3 assets in the snapshot across incretin, lipoprotein(a), oncology and Alzheimer's [CLM-012000] [CLM-012021].
- J&J: five novel approvals (two bispecifics, an oral peptide, a TKI, an FcRn antibody) plus a bispecific/CAR-T oncology Phase 3 stack [CLM-012008] [CLM-012012].
- AbbVie: five novel approvals but the 2022-2025 growth came from Skyrizi/Rinvoq supplements [CLM-012033] [CLM-012036]; the 2023-2025 setbacks (ABBV-951 CRLs, emraclidine, navitoclax, VERONA) were larger in count than at peers [CLM-012116] [CLM-012117].
- Takeda: three novel approvals, all arriving in 2023 or 2026; two 2026 approvals plus zasocitinib Phase 3 form the replacement set [CLM-012019] [CLM-012131].

## Concentration

- Lilly is the most concentrated: Mounjaro and Zepbound were 56% of 2025 revenue [CLM-012124], and the Phase 3 list is dominated by incretin and incretin-adjacent assets (retatrutide, orforglipron, eloralintide, tirzepatide outcomes).
- J&J and AbbVie are concentrated in immunology (Tremfya, Skyrizi, Rinvoq) and oncology respectively; Stelara biosimilar erosion is described in J&J's FY2025 10-K *(not registered as a claim; gap)* and replacement sizing was not retrieved.

## Modality mix

Broadest at J&J (bispecifics, CAR-T, oral peptide, drug-device, FcRn antibody) and AbbVie (ADCs, bispecific antibody, small molecules); Lilly spans peptides, oral small molecules and siRNA (lepodisiran) [CLM-012140]; Takeda spans oral small molecules, peptides, an siRNA and an antibody, having exited cell therapy [CLM-012123]. Gene editing and radioligand programmes were not evidenced.

## Revenue-replacement adequacy

- Lilly: the pipeline mostly extends the franchise that already supplies the revenue (oral and multi-agonist incretins), so replacement need is low near term and the risk is concentration, not shortage; orforglipron's voucher and 2026-04-01 approval support timely launch [CLM-012125] [CLM-012006].
- J&J: Stelara is exposed to biosimilars; Tremfya, Icotyde and the bispecific/CAR-T stack are the identified successors, with Icotyde approved 2026-03-17 [CLM-012012]. Adequacy cannot be sized without revenue forecasts (gap).
- AbbVie: Humira revenue fell sharply in 2025 and is offset by Skyrizi and Rinvoq *(gap: the FY2025 10-K revenue table was read but not registered as a claim)*; the novel pipeline (tavapadon, ADCs) is smaller than the lost revenue, so growth depends on immunology supplements and label expansion *(INTERPRETATION)*; Rinvoq GCA supplement [CLM-012036].
- Takeda: Exkivity withdrawal, Alofisel and cell-therapy impairments, and soticlestat failure removed several late assets [CLM-012121] [CLM-012120]; oveporexton and zasocitinib are the principal replacement bets.

Confidence: moderate on counts and dates (regulator records), low on adequacy (no forecast data retrieved).
