# Scientific platform capabilities (evidence-based)

Each capability is an INTERPRETATION built from regulator, registry and peer-reviewed artefacts. Capability is not commercial value. Company-claimed platforms with no such artefact retrieved are excluded.

## Eli Lilly

- Lilly platform capability (oral small-molecule incretin chemistry): orforglipron is an oral GLP-1 receptor agonist that reached an FDA approval (Foundayo, 2026-04-01) and has an ATTAIN Phase 3 publication programme including a JAMA report in obstructive sleep apnea. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012138]
- Lilly platform capability (multi-agonist peptides): retatrutide, a GIP/GLP-1/glucagon triple agonist, has Phase 2 (NEJM 2023) and TRIUMPH-1 Phase 3 (NEJM, 2026-11-05) publications and a 10,000-participant outcomes trial. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012139]
- Lilly platform capability (RNA therapeutics): lepodisiran, an siRNA against lipoprotein(a), is in the ACCLAIM-Lp(a) cardiovascular outcomes Phase 3 (registry record NCT06292013, about 17,300 participants). *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012140]
- Lilly platform capability (precision oncology small molecules): Lilly (via Loxo Oncology) obtained FDA approval of Jaypirca in 2023 and converted CLL/SLL from accelerated to traditional approval on 2025-12-02, and Inluriyo was approved 2025-09-25. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012141]

## Johnson & Johnson

- J&J platform capability (T-cell-engaging bispecifics): Tecvayli (2022-10-25) and Talvey (2023-08-09) were both approved by FDA, Tecvayli's pivotal MajesTEC-1 was published in NEJM, and MajesTEC-7 Phase 3 (NCT05552222) combines the two engagers. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012142]
- J&J platform capability (oral targeted peptides): icotrokinra, an oral IL-23 receptor peptide, was approved as Icotyde on 2026-03-17 and its Phase 3 results are published in NEJM (2025-11-06). *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012143]
- J&J platform capability (CAR-T and bispecific oncology): cilta-cel CARTITUDE-4 was published in NEJM (2023-07-27) and CARTITUDE-5 Phase 3 (NCT04923893) has an estimated primary completion of 2029-02-14. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012144]
- J&J platform capability (EGFR/MET bispecific plus TKI combination): Rybrevant plus Lazcluze received first-line FDA approval on 2024-08-19 supported by MARIPOSA (NEJM 2024), and an OrigAMI-2 colorectal Phase 3 (NCT06662786) is recruiting. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012145]

## AbbVie

- AbbVie platform capability (antibody-drug conjugates): FDA records show Elahere (2022-11-14, sponsor ImmunoGen) and Emrelis (2025-05-14, AbbVie) approvals, Decnupaz (2026-05-27) approval, and a Temab-A Phase 2/3 with osimertinib (NCT07005102). *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012146]
- AbbVie platform capability (immunology lifecycle management): FDA approved Skyrizi in Crohn's disease (2022-06-16) and ulcerative colitis (2024-06-18) and Rinvoq in atopic dermatitis (2022-01-14), ulcerative colitis (2022-03-16) and giant cell arteritis (2025-04-28). *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012147]
- AbbVie neuroscience capability: tavapadon (D1/D5 partial agonist) has a published fixed-dose Phase 3 (JAMA Neurol 2026), an NDA submitted September 2025 and FDA approval as Juvmo on 2026-09-25, while emraclidine failed Phase 2. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012148]

## Takeda

- Takeda platform capability (orexin biology): oveporexton, an oral orexin-2 agonist, has NEJM Phase 2 (2025) and Phase 3 (2026-09-24) publications and was approved as Orzeyful on 2026-08-05. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012149]
- Takeda platform capability (oral TYK2 inhibition): zasocitinib has a Phase 2 publication (JAMA Dermatol 2024), two completed Phase 3 psoriasis registry entries, and a completed head-to-head Phase 3 versus deucravacitinib (NCT06973291). *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012150]
- Takeda platform capability (in-licensed first-in-class assets): rusfertide (hepcidin mimetic, Protagonist) has a Phase 3 VERIFY publication in J Clin Oncol (2026-10) and FDA approval as Mimrylo on 2026-08-28. *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012151]
- Takeda platform capability (RNAi): fazirsiran, a GalNAc siRNA, is in Phase 3 for alpha-1 antitrypsin liver disease (NCT05677971, primary completion estimated 2029-01-17). *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-012152]

Not evidenced in this run (gap): Lilly radioligand (POINT) and gene editing (Verve), J&J ADC via Ambrx and Legend relationship detail, AbbVie in-vivo CAR-T (Capstan), Takeda cell-therapy exit rationale beyond the impairment disclosure [CLM-012123].
