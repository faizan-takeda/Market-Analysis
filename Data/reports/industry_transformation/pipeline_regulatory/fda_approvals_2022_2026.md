## What the approvals show *(INTERPRETATION)*

- Count of CDER novel approvals in window: Lilly 8, J&J 5, AbbVie 5, Takeda 3 (Takeda's two 2026 approvals are in-licensed or newly launched orexin and hepcidin-mimetic assets).
- Lilly's approvals span incretin, immunology, neurology and oncology, but the revenue-bearing ones are Mounjaro [CLM-012000] and the Zepbound NDA [CLM-012021].
- AbbVie's volume growth in the window rests on supplemental indications for Skyrizi and Rinvoq (section C), not on novel approvals.
