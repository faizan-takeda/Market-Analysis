# Autonomous Lab Reality Check (A6, wave 1)

Question: what is technically and commercially mature today in laboratory automation and "autonomous" (closed-loop, AI-directed) discovery, judged only by evidence opened for this review? Cutoff 2026-11-21.

## What is established
- **Lilly built and then divested an open robotic cloud lab.** The Life Sciences Studio in San Diego (public launch about 2020, with Strateos) was acquired by Arctoris in 2024 and relocated to Oxford, with five biochemistry modules, a high-throughput screening module, a BSL2 cell-biology module and storage for 4 million compounds [CLM-006013]. Cost (USD 90 million) and start year (2017 versus about 2020) are reported inconsistently [CLM-006014]. No disclosed discovery outcome came from it in any source opened.
- **The asset continues as a service.** Arctoris (a CRO) runs the platform, expanded its biophysics capacity ten-fold and was selected for a UK ARIA AI Scientist programme [CLM-006016]. Automation is being sold as data generation for AI customers, not as an autonomous discovery engine.
- **Biofoundry automation plus ML did not guarantee commercial success.** Zymergen sold to Ginkgo for about USD 300 million, one-tenth of its roughly USD 3 billion IPO-era valuation, after cumulative net losses of USD 1.2 billion and a discontinued first product [CLM-006051].
- **Lilly's next step is an announcement.** The NVIDIA–Lilly co-innovation lab (up to USD 1 billion over five years) targets a "continuous learning system" linking agentic wet labs with dry labs; it is at concept stage [CLM-000281]. LillyPod is operational as compute, with no reported result [CLM-006004, CLM-006007].
- **Physical automation that is operating is conventional.** The Lilly Lebanon Advanced Therapies plant has robotic machines that dose, measure and seal medicines, seen on a guided tour with no performance data [CLM-006002].
- **A fast-feedback AI-wet-lab loop is claimed by vendors.** Nabla says design-to-test takes three to four weeks and expects first-in-human data within one to two years (both company statements) [CLM-006039, CLM-006040]; BigHat pairs ML with a high-speed wet lab under an AbbVie deal with no result yet [CLM-006035].

## What is contested
- Whether Lilly sold the Studio because the model failed, because utilisation was low or for portfolio reasons: the reason is undisclosed [CLM-006017].
- Whether Strateos closed or was acquired: one unverified aggregator snippet says it was acquired by Multiply Labs in December 2023; no primary source opened, so the research brief's "closure" is not confirmed [CLM-006015].
- Whether AI-originated molecules outperform conventional ones clinically. Rentosertib (Insilico) shows a peer-reviewed Phase IIa FVC signal with liver-safety discontinuations [CLM-006048]; Recursion ended four of 11 programmes in 2025 [CLM-006047]; the cited aggregate Phase 2 success rate for AI-originated compounds is about the same as conventional (secondary citation) [CLM-006049].

## What is unknown
- Any peer-reviewed or audited result from a fully closed-loop autonomous lab at any of the four companies.
- Utilisation, cost per experiment or error-rate data for Lilly's or any pharma's robotic labs.
- Whether Lilly's co-innovation lab has begun operations.
- Emerald Cloud Lab and other cloud-lab track records (not investigated).

## Technology readiness judgement (INTERPRETATION)
| Layer | Readiness (evidence-based) | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| High-throughput, scripted automation (screening, dosing, fill-finish) | operational, commodity | Arctoris platform; Lilly plant [CLM-006013, CLM-006002] |
| Remote/cloud access to a robotic lab | operational but commercially fragile | Studio divested; platform now CRO-run [CLM-006017, CLM-006016] |
| AI-planned experiments in an owned wet-lab loop | prototype to pilot, unproven | Nabla/BigHat claims; co-innovation lab concept [CLM-006040, CLM-006035, CLM-000281] |
| "Autonomous" end-to-end discovery with clinical outcome | not demonstrated | No source opened shows it |

## Implications for Takeda (RECOMMENDATION)
Rent automation and data generation (CRO model) rather than build an owned robotic lab; gate AI-biotech deals on first-in-human data; revisit when an independent outcome from Lilly's lab programme appears [CLM-006052, CLM-006058].
