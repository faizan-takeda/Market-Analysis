# AI, Data & Robotics Landscape: Lilly, J&J, AbbVie, Takeda (A6, wave 1)

Window 2021-11 to 2026-11-21. Every claim carries maturity in the shard (`shards/A6_ai_robotics/claims.jsonl`). A press release proves an announcement, not an outcome. Cards: [capability_mechanism_cards.md](capability_mechanism_cards.md). Matrix: [maturity_matrix.md](maturity_matrix.md). Labs: [autonomous_lab_reality_check.md](autonomous_lab_reality_check.md).

## 1. Summary
- Lilly has the largest and best-evidenced build in infrastructure terms: an operating 1,016-GPU cluster [CLM-006004], a live data-as-product platform (TuneLab) [CLM-000280], and several external model partners [CLM-006012, CLM-006009, CLM-006010]. No source opened shows an outcome from any of it [CLM-006007].
- J&J has the only regulator-verified robotics milestones (OTTAVA De Novo; MONARCH QUEST 3 clearance) [CLM-000283, CLM-006027], plus self-reported enterprise AI metrics [CLM-006020]. OTTAVA has one announced installation [CLM-000284].
- AbbVie built an internal knowledge-graph platform (ARCH) with candid cost commentary but no outcome beyond a preprint [CLM-006032, CLM-006033], and buys AI through BigHat and Valkai [CLM-006035, CLM-000285].
- Takeda's disclosed AI strategy is external partnering (Nabla, Iambic, AISB) at prototype stage [CLM-006039, CLM-006041, CLM-006042]; no first-party deployment with metrics was found [CLM-006045].
- Industry calibration: AI-platform pipelines still suffer ordinary attrition [CLM-006047], the best public AI-origin clinical data are a small Phase IIa [CLM-006048], and owned automation ventures have ended in divestment or distressed sale [CLM-006052].

## 2. Eli Lilly

**Established**
- *Compute and data.* Lilly's AI factory is a DGX SuperPOD (B300) with 1,016 Blackwell Ultra GPUs [CLM-006003], inaugurated about February 2026, assembled in about four months, over 9,000 petaflops [CLM-006004]. TuneLab, launched 2025-09-09, offers Lilly-trained models to biotechs via federated learning (company estimates over USD 1 billion of underlying data cost) [CLM-000280]; Benchling will distribute it to 1,300+ customers, opt-in later in 2026 [CLM-006008]. Maturity: operational (LillyPod, TuneLab), pilot (distribution).
- *External models.* Isomorphic (USD 45 million upfront, up to USD 1.7 billion) [CLM-006012]; insitro ADMET models [CLM-006009]; Chai custom antibody model (trade report) [CLM-006010]; OpenAI antimicrobials, announced June 2024, not September 2025 as the brief states [CLM-006011]. Maturity: concept to prototype.
- *Physical.* Lebanon Advanced Therapies opened 2026-05-06 with robotic dosing/sealing seen on a tour [CLM-006002]; the USD 4.5 billion Medicine Foundry remains planned for late 2027 [CLM-006000, CLM-006001]. NVIDIA describes plant robots and digital twins without counts [CLM-006006]. Lilly's capex was USD 7.8 billion in 2025 [CLM-000246].
- *Retreat.* The Life Sciences Studio / Strateos remote robotic lab was sold to Arctoris in 2024 [CLM-006013]; a reported USD 90 million build cost conflicts with another source on dates [CLM-006014].

**Contested:** the reason for divesting the lab [CLM-006017]; whether Strateos closed or was acquired [CLM-006015]; the Chai hit-rate claim [CLM-006010].

**Unknown:** any result from LillyPod, TuneLab uptake, co-innovation lab status (concept at CLM-000281), Lilly Gateway Labs/Catalyze360 outcomes (not assessed), and Lilly's 10-K quantifies no AI outcome [CLM-000252].

## 3. Johnson & Johnson

**Established**
- *Enterprise AI.* J&J's CIO said nearly 900 gen-AI use cases were reviewed and the company moved from proofs of concept to deployment [CLM-006018]; 10–15% of use cases drive about 80% of value [CLM-006019]. Self-reported results: "up to 2.6x" trial-site enrolment [CLM-006020], quality-control answers in minutes [CLM-006021], 47,000 employees through a required gen-AI course [CLM-006022]. Maturity: pilot to operational; outcome evidence none independent.
- *OTTAVA.* De Novo 2026-07-22 [CLM-000283], first installation 2026-11-19 [CLM-000284]; IDE trial fitted ORs as small as 243 sq ft, efficiency survey n=20 [CLM-006028]. Maturity: pilot. FY2025 10-K contains no OTTAVA or MONARCH text [CLM-006025].
- *MONARCH.* QUEST 3 clearance announced 2026-08-17 with AI nodule segmentation [CLM-006027]. Maturity: operational.
- *VELYS.* Only a 10-K statement of pull-through for ATTUNE knees [CLM-006026].
- *Polyphonic.* "Early access to select hospitals only" [CLM-006030]; Abu Dhabi network deployment "expected" [CLM-006031].
- *Federated structure model.* J&J is a founding AISB member [CLM-006042].

**Contested:** executive-reported metrics lack baselines (WSJ original paywalled) [CLM-006019].

**Unknown:** VELYS and MONARCH installed bases, OTTAVA revenue, Polyphonic hospital count. Varipulse was reviewed and excluded as not an AI/robotics initiative.

## 4. AbbVie

**Established**
- ARCH: conceived 2020, built by fewer than ten FTE plus contractors, harmonised data over 124 TB, graph of over 30 million nodes and nearly 2 billion relationships, access for all R&D [CLM-006032, CLM-006034]; cost undisclosed, built "quick, not cheap, not right [or final]" [CLM-006033]. Maturity: operational. The source describes a knowledge graph, not generative AI, contrary to the brief.
- BigHat: USD 30 million upfront, up to about USD 325 million milestones [CLM-006035] (prototype). Valkai: announcement only [CLM-006037] (concept).
- 10-K discloses AI skills investment and an adoption-failure risk, no outcomes [CLM-006036].
- AISB founding member [CLM-006042] and AISB Bind partner [CLM-006044].

**Unknown:** ARCH outcomes (preprint not opened), Allergan Aesthetics digital and manufacturing automation (not searched in depth).

## 5. Takeda (baseline)
- Nabla Bio second collaboration, October 2025: double-digit millions upfront and research, over USD 1 billion success-based [CLM-006039]; first-in-human expected in one to two years [CLM-006039]. Iambic, February 2026: success-based payments could exceed USD 1.7 billion [CLM-006041]. AISB member since October 2025 [CLM-006042, CLM-006043]; not listed in AISB Bind [CLM-006044]. 20-F lists digital transformation and AI risks, no quantified outcome [CLM-000278]; BioLife plasma centres grew to 260 [CLM-000279]. Maturity: prototype to pilot for all named deals [CLM-006045].

## 6. Industry calibration
- FDA draft guidance (January 2025) sets a risk-based credibility framework for AI used in regulatory decisions [CLM-006046].
- Recursion ended four of 11 programmes in 2025 [CLM-006047]; Insilico's rentosertib Phase IIa showed +98.4 mL FVC with liver-related discontinuations [CLM-006048]; Phase III start is an unverified snippet [CLM-006050]; AI-origin Phase 2 success is cited as about 40%, similar to conventional drugs [CLM-006049].
- Zymergen: sold at about one-tenth of IPO valuation [CLM-006051]. Automation track record: [autonomous_lab_reality_check.md](autonomous_lab_reality_check.md) [CLM-006052].

## 7. Cross-company reading (INTERPRETATION)
- Posture: Lilly builds and partners; J&J pursues productivity AI and device robotics; AbbVie internalises data and buys models; Takeda partners [CLM-006053].
- Only J&J's devices have regulator-verified milestones; no discovery or financial outcome is public for the four companies [CLM-006054].
- Lilly's defensibility rests on data, not compute [CLM-006055]; federated consortia are the common mechanism for pooling pharma data [CLM-006056].
- Cost visibility is limited to deal terms and a few capex numbers [CLM-006057].

## 8. Takeda options (RECOMMENDATION)
Partner and watch; gate deals on first-in-human data; use CRO automation; do not match Lilly's compute build [CLM-006058, CLM-006059].
