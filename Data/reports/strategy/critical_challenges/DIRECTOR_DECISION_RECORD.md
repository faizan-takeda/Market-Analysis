## Net effect (ASSUMPTION-basis figures)
Balanced envelope USD 2,950m → **~1,920m committed-at-gates**, with ~1,030m contingent headroom reserved for the licensing channel (BET-18) — not a saving. Core H1 2027–29 tranche ≈ USD 465m. Capital-constrained and ambitious portfolios are rescaled proportionally by A11c-bis and re-labelled v2.

## Not accepted / deferred
- The red team's opposition to any plasma spend (keep EXPERIMENT, not REJECT): the baseline/shadow-mode tranche is cheap and produces the data the council says is missing.
- Removing BET-03 entirely: orexin is Takeda's only evidenced in-house discovery success in the window (A5); the smaller gated size is the compromise.
