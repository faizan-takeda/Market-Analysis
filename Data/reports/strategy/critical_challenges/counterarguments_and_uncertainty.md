## Unsupported assumptions

- **CA-001 (THEME).** ASM-024 (Entyvio erosion of 40-80% over three years) is bracketed by Eylea (-42% in one year) and Humira (-78.6% over three years). Both are different products with different channels. Neither is an intravenous infused biologic, so the bracket has no analogue for Entyvio's actual route of administration. [CLM-011017] [CLM-003002] (ASM-024)
- **CA-002 (THEME).** ASM-025 puts first meaningful US Entyvio biosimilar entry at 2031 (range 2030-2032). The range excludes entry in 2027-2029. Two biosimilar BLAs are under FDA review (one decision expected Q1 2027 per trade reporting), the reference product was first approved on 2014-05-20, and Takeda says biosimilars launching before the 2032 patent dates must address infringement and validity. Under the statutory 12-year reference-product exclusivity (a rule outside the registry, to be verified), the regulatory barrier would already have lapsed in May 2026, leaving patents as the only barrier. Playbook milestone RM-026 (2029 H2) would be late if entry were earlier. [CLM-005017] [CLM-014050] [CLM-014051] (ASM-025)
- **CA-003 (THEME).** The Entyvio exposure arithmetic applies ASM-024 to the global JPY 958.0bn. The US was JPY 351.8bn of JPY 541.7bn Entyvio sales in H1 FY2026 (about 65%, council arithmetic). ## Failed strategic hypotheses (competitor and Takeda failures that warn against a bet)

[CLM-006013] [CLM-006016] [CLM-006017]

## Alternative interpretations

- **CA-075 (BET-09).** Lilly's 2024 sale of the Studio and its 2026 joint lab with NVIDIA (up to USD 1bn) can be read as re-platforming at larger scale, which reverses the lesson the strategy draws ('owned labs fail'). Under that reading renting is a way to be a follower. [CLM-006013] [CLM-000281] [CLM-006017]
- **CA-076 (BET-04).** Entyvio's FY2025 growth (+4.8%) and H1 FY2026 growth (+2.7% CER) can be read as proof the franchise is resilient before entry, in which case early spend is premature. The dated exposure argues the reverse. The registry cannot settle which reading holds. [CLM-000274] [CLM-005012]
- **CA-077 (BET-04).** The Humira lesson can be read two ways: contracting retained volume (97% for 14 months), or contracting failed because net price collapsed anyway. The strategy uses it as a model for defence; a contrary reading treats it as a warning that volume defence is not revenue defence. [CLM-003007] [CLM-003010] [CLM-003002]
- **CA-078 (BET-02).** Plasma-derived revenue -1.5% at constant rates in H1 FY2026 can mean a mature, demand-bound category (automation saves cost only) rather than a capacity-bound one (automation unlocks revenue). The strategy's 'capacity' reading is favourable to the bet. [CLM-005064] [CLM-005065]
- **CA-079 (BET-05).** The strategy treats zasocitinib as replacement revenue. It can equally be read as a second concentration risk: a single oral TYK2 product with a USD 4bn price carries two USD 1bn milestones and enters a class where J&J has an approved oral comparator. [CLM-009076] [CLM-012012] [CLM-002065]
- **CA-080 (BET-06).** The rationale 'options are safer than purchases' can be reversed: AbbVie's option stream and Lilly's expensed IPR&D produced no approvals by the cutoff, while Takeda's approved late-stage assets came from licensed Phase 2/3 programmes (Protagonist, Nimbus). - **CA-081 (BET-07).** Takeda's AI partnerships may be better read as data bets than as AI bets: the scarce input is proprietary assay and clinical data, and the partner model is replaceable. That makes BET-07 part of BET-01 and BET-09. [CLM-006055] [CLM-006056] [CLM-011087]
