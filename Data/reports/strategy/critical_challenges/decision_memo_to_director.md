# Decision memo to the Director: proposed changes to the 17 bets (A11e, wave 5)

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. From: independent Strategic Council and red team (A11e). To: Director. The council reviewed all 17 bets in `strategy/data/strategy_bets.json` with seven councils (scientific, technology, commercial, capital allocation, regulatory and ethics, competitive red team, Takeda execution). Details: [council_reviews.md](council_reviews.md), [red_team.md](red_team.md), [scenario_stress_test.md](scenario_stress_test.md), [counterarguments_and_uncertainty.md](counterarguments_and_uncertainty.md). Bets, portfolios and roadmap were not edited; every change below is a proposal. All dollar figures are the strategy's ASSUMPTIONs (IVB-014: Takeda's budget is not public). Council arithmetic is marked.

## 1. Summary

Over 119 reviews the councils returned 45 support, 64 conditional and 10 oppose. The decisions the councils back without material change are BET-05, BET-08, BET-09, BET-10, BET-14, BET-16 and, with conditions, BET-01 and BET-17. Ten changes are proposed, ranked by consequence for the Entyvio window (2031-2034 under ASM-025) and strength of evidence.

**Headline finding.** The portfolio spends 49.5% of its balanced envelope (USD 1,460m of 2,950m: BET-03, 06, 07 and 13) on bets whose own evaluation puts payoff at eight years or more, after the Entyvio erosion window, while the instrument Takeda has actually used to refill its pipeline (mid-size Phase 2/3 licences: Nimbus USD 4.0bn, Innovent USD 1.2bn upfront, Protagonist, Keros, Arrowhead) has no funded bet. Four of six late-stage programmes were externally sourced [CLM-005050] [CLM-009076] [CLM-009078]. BET-15 (USD 5bn or more) is the only bridge in the set and fails the strategy's own leverage test by arithmetic.

## 2. Ranked proposed changes

### 1. BET-15 DEFER becomes REJECT as framed; create a funded mid-size licensing channel (proposed BET-18)

- **Change.** Replace the binary USD 5bn trigger with a standing channel for Phase 2/3 assets with a per-deal upfront ceiling (council suggestion: USD 0.5-2bn, to be set after IVB-012). Funding stays outside the envelope until Amitiza cash is clear.
- **Why.** At JPY 150 per USD, USD 5bn is JPY 750bn. Adjusted net debt of JPY 3,817.6bn over EBITDA JPY 1,457.2bn is 2.6x; adding the purchase gives about 3.13x, and about 3.41x if the JPY 403.5bn Amitiza provision is also debt-funded, against the strategy's 3.0x ceiling (council arithmetic; free cash flow and acquired earnings ignored) [CLM-005005] [CLM-005014]. AbbVie bought bridges of USD 17.5bn net in 2024 and J&J USD 17.5bn in FY2025 [CLM-003086] [CLM-009049]. Entyvio exposure is JPY 383-766bn a year at ASM-024 and BET-05 breadth is USD 180m.
- **Dissent.** Capital council: conditional (agrees nothing is funded now). Red team: oppose DEFER (waiting loses assets). Takeda execution: conditional on leverage policy and the new CEO.
- **Would change my mind.** IVB-012 showing the provision is already in adjusted net debt and free cash flow deleverages below 3.0x within 24 months.

### 2. Add an early-entry case and re-date the Entyvio playbook (BET-04, ASM-025, RM-026)

- **Change.** Widen ASM-025 to 2028-2032, add council stress case S-A to the scenario set, and move RM-026 (playbook live) from 2029 H2 to 2028 H1. No extra capital.
- **Why.** Two biosimilar BLAs are under FDA review, one decision expected Q1 2027 per trade reporting; the reference product was approved on 2014-05-20; Takeda says earlier launches must address patent infringement and validity (patents expire in some cases in 2032) and third-party revocation actions were filed in the UK and Netherlands in February-April 2026 [CLM-014051] [CLM-014050] [CLM-005017]. The statutory 12-year exclusivity period (a rule outside the registry, to be verified) would already have ended in May 2026. The current range (2030-2032) excludes at-risk or settlement entry.
- **Dissent.** None recorded; unresolved: no Purple Book or US litigation record in the registry.

### 3. BET-06 PARTNER (USD 700m) becomes a scorecard-first engine at the constrained size (USD 300m), with the balance contingent

- **Change.** Fund USD 300m (the strategy's own constrained size), release the remainder only on RM-007 (deal-outcome scorecard, 2027 H1) and the first two deals.
- **Why.** No option from the window produced an approved product; per-asset probability is 0.05-0.15; first option-sourced approval is 2035 (RM-043), so it cannot bridge 2031-2034 [CLM-009097]. The engine is smaller than one Innovent deal (USD 1.2bn upfront), so it neither funds the real path (change 1) nor wins bidding contests against AbbVie (ADARx USD 335m upfront) [CLM-009078] [CLM-009061]. Neuroscience, gene and cell therapy concentrate write-downs, and Takeda exited cell therapy at JPY 58.2bn [CLM-009100] [CLM-009084].
- **Dissent.** Takeda execution: conditional (deal flow is a public strength, but the early-option organisation is undocumented, IVB-007).
- **Saves.** USD 400m of balanced envelope.

### 4. BET-02 BUILD becomes EXPERIMENT until a measured baseline and a plasma-peer benchmark exist

- **Change.** Commit only the baseline and shadow-mode tranche (strategy gates: USD 20m discovery plus USD 45m feasibility, USD 65m at risk) and re-base ASM-031 on money (the value of a 2% gain per litre) before the USD 365m remainder is released. Add a test for whether plasma is demand- or capacity-bound.
- **Why.** H1 FY2026 plasma revenue was +8.5% at actual rates and -1.5% at constant rates, which does not show a capacity constraint [CLM-005064]. The 'no competitor holds this data' premise is true only because the comparison set has no plasma peers; the registry holds none [CLM-011056] [CLM-009091]. No public evidence of Takeda manufacturing robotics, a prior warning letter and shortage, USD 230m of expansion without commissioning data, and fixed-price vendor terms are a NEEDS_MORE_RESEARCH hypothesis [CLM-005067] [CLM-005020] [CLM-005065] [CLM-011031].
- **Dissent.** Takeda execution: support (the CEO led the Plasma-Derived Therapies unit, 270+ centres, 99.6% on-time in-full) [CLM-005074] [CLM-005063] [CLM-014046]. Red team: oppose the data-moat premise.
- **Saves.** Up to USD 200m at the constrained size (USD 230m); the council proposes the constrained size as the ceiling after the baseline.

### 5. BET-03 BUILD (USD 500m) becomes BUILD at USD 300m with a Phase 1 gate before the H2 tranche

- **Change.** Fund the constrained size and release the next tranche only on a clean second candidate through Phase 1 (RM-030, target 2030 H1). Before H2, require an additionality test against base R&D and a competitor orexin scan.
- **Why.** Second-asset approval is ASM-021 at 15% (range 5-30%), payoff 8-12 years, platform proof 2038 (RM-048). The orexin discovery team is already in base R&D (JPY 675.9bn), so the USD 500m's incrementality cannot be tested [CLM-005004] [CLM-005048]. TAK-994 was stopped for a liver signal, soticlestat missed Phase 3, and Orzeyful's launch awaits a DEA scheduling decision [CLM-012119] [CLM-012120] [CLM-005029]. Competitor orexin programmes are not in the registry.
- **Dissent.** Technology and Takeda execution: support (proven in-house capability).
- **Saves.** USD 200m.

### 6. BET-04 BUILD stays; add a benefit-channel gate and reframe the outcome from 'retain revenue' to 'delay and measure'

- **Change.** Make IVB-004 (RM-003, 2027 H1) a hard gate: if most Entyvio volume sits outside the contractable pharmacy channel, redirect part of the USD 140m to provider and site-of-care strategy. Replace 'retain 10 points of erosion' with measured net price and persistence versus the ASM-024 low case.
- **Why.** Humira kept at least 97% of volume to March 2024 through rebate-for-access and still fell 78.6% in three years; US Humira fell 41% in 2024 and 57% in 2025 [CLM-003007] [CLM-003002] [CLM-003010]. AbbVie and J&J already sell competing IBD products [CLM-003016] [CLM-002053]. PBMs prefer private-label biosimilars [CLM-007015] [CLM-007016]. The Medicare price from January 2028 is statutory [CLM-007026]. The registry has no Entyvio medical-benefit versus pharmacy-benefit split.
- **Dissent.** Commercial and Capital: support as written. Red team: conditional.

### 7. BET-07 PARTNER: keep, but re-wire the prerequisites and treat it as a feature of BET-01 and BET-09

- **Change.** Either make roadmap gate RM-011 (Nabla first-in-human window, 2027 H2) depend on a comparator (BET-01 decision log RM-015 and BET-09 cost benchmark RM-018, 2028 H1), or drop BET-01 and BET-09 from BET-07's hard prerequisites in the bets file. Release USD 80m H2 and USD 60m H3 only at RM-020 (2028 H2). Write stop rules into contracts before the next payment.
- **Why.** The bets file lists BET-01 and BET-09 as hard prerequisites but RM-011 depends only on RM-007, so first-in-human data can arrive before a conventional-candidate comparator exists. Evidence of AI-origin advantage is absent or mixed [CLM-006047] [CLM-006048] [CLM-006049]. Rivals hold equal or larger alliances (Isomorphic, Chai, OpenAI, BigHat, Valkai) and the AISB fine-tune is shared [CLM-006012] [CLM-006010] [CLM-006011] [CLM-006035] [CLM-000285] [CLM-006043].
- **Dissent.** Capital: conditional (a fair option price). Commercial and Red team: oppose the differentiation claim.
- **Saves.** USD 100m contingent (constrained size USD 80m).

### 8. BET-11 BUILD stays but resolve its conflict with BET-04 and relabel confidence

- **Change.** Baseline first (RM-013), then device spend. Decide, before scaling SC, whether moving patients into the pharmacy benefit helps or hurts after biosimilar entry. Relabel `confidence` from evidence_backed to hypothesis.
- **Why.** The supporting claim is PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED and states that the effect after entry is not established [CLM-007053]. Stelara's biosimilars moved through PBM templates in 2025-2026 [CLM-007015] [CLM-007016]. Tremfya and Skyrizi are already convenient [CLM-002026] [CLM-003016].

### 9. BET-12 EXPERIMENT (USD 100m) capped at USD 50m, aligned to one launch

- **Change.** Attach the pilot to oveporexton with a named leader and bring the start before the 2027 H2 date in RM-012; hold H2 and H3 USD 70m for RM-021 evidence.
- **Why.** Orzeyful was approved on 2026-08-05 and the pilot would start about a year later and report in 2028 H2, after the launch curve forms [CLM-005029]. No company publishes persistence or time-to-therapy outcomes [CLM-007039]. Owner sits in the same US organisation carrying three launches and the January 2028 Entyvio price [CLM-005074] [CLM-007033].
- **Saves.** USD 50m.

### 10. Housekeeping that changes decisions at the margin

- **BET-13:** remove the USD 80m balanced carry (a DEFER bet is funded while BET-15 is not). Fund a USD 25m options paper (RM-028) from BET-12. Saves USD 80m.
- **BET-17:** move ownership of measurement from the CFO, who owns the JPY 200bn savings target, to a function independent of that target (council suggestion: internal audit or the CDTO with Audit Committee visibility) so that IVB-003 attribution is credible [CLM-005059] [CLM-005078].
- **Confidence labels:** relabel BET-08 and BET-09 (and BET-11, above) from evidence_backed to hypothesis where the load-bearing claims are PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED or NEEDS_MORE_RESEARCH [CLM-011054] [CLM-011065] [CLM-011066].
- **Text errors:** 'CVS removed Zepbound for about 11 months [CLM-007011]' should read about 15 months and cite [CLM-007013] (restored 1 October 2026); the claim records an 11% share-price fall. Stage-gate capital at risk exceeds the bet size by USD 5m for BET-02, 07, 09 and 11.

## 4. Decisions the councils back

BET-05 INVEST (best clinical evidence in the set), BET-08 PARTNER (near-free), BET-09 PARTNER (rent, not own, with the caveat that the 'owned labs fail' evidence is weaker than stated), BET-10 REJECT, BET-14 REJECT and BET-16 REJECT (all seven councils endorse BET-14 and BET-16; BET-10 has one conditional from the red team), BET-01 BUILD as a thin slice, and BET-17 EXPERIMENT with the owner change.

## 5. Questions only the Director can settle

1. Is the licensing channel (change 1) in scope for this strategy, or is it a corporate-development decision outside it? The council cannot size it without IVB-012 and the leverage policy.
2. Does the Director accept the council's SCN-3 view that no funded bet is robust to pricing pressure on evidence (the strategy rates seven robust)? If yes, the strategy needs a lever beyond contracting for the Entyvio price.
3. How to resolve the BET-15 disagreement: the red team says waiting loses assets to AbbVie and J&J, the capital council says leverage already fails the 3.0x test by arithmetic. The council did not harmonise these.
4. Whether internal data (IVB-001, IVB-004, IVB-006, IVB-012) can be obtained before the first gate decisions in 2027 H1; four of the ten changes turn on them.
