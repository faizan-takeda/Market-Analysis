# Scenario stress test (A11e, wave 5)

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. The five scenarios are those in `strategy/data/scenarios.json` (SCN-1 to SCN-5). They are SCENARIOs, not forecasts. The strategy's robustness table is reproduced and re-rated by the council. Where the council differs the cell is marked and the reason is given. Capital figures are the strategy's balanced-portfolio ASSUMPTIONs (cumulative 2027-2040, USD m); Takeda's budget is not public (IVB-014). Council arithmetic is derived from registry values and ASM-001 (JPY 150 per USD).

## 1. Scenario recap and what each stresses

- **SCN-1 AI materially accelerates R&D.** SCENARIO: by about 2031 independent evidence shows AI-planned discovery and development cutting cycle time or raising hit rate at several companies; first-in-human data from AI-designed molecules are positive; compute and models commoditise further.
- **SCN-2 AI incremental.** SCENARIO: AI delivers productivity gains in documents, operations and decision support but no step change in probability of success or cycle time; no independent outcome appears for platform investments.
- **SCN-3 Pricing, regulatory and biosimilar pressure intensifies.** SCENARIO: Medicare negotiation, most-favoured-nation pricing and PBM private-label biosimilars compress net price faster; Entyvio erosion lands at the high end of ASM-024; Takhzyro and others face negotiated prices.
- **SCN-4 New modalities, care models and tech-native competitors reshape value.** SCENARIO: in-vivo cell therapy, siRNA and gene editing move into the clinic; home and digital care models change where value accrues; AI-native biotechs and tech platforms (Isomorphic-type) win partnerships; platforms do not protect single products.
- **SCN-5 Combined downside.** SCENARIO: SCN-2 and SCN-3 together, plus launch slippage for 2026 approvals (inspection findings, payer delays) and Amitiza cash outflow near the full provision; leverage stays near 3x and discretionary spend is cut.

The council re-rated 19 of 85 cells (22%). Reasons:

- **BET-01 in SCN-5: neutral to fragile.** First discretionary cut in a cash squeeze; only the thin slice (metadata standard, decision log) is worth protecting.
- **BET-02 in SCN-3: robust to neutral.** Immunoglobulin exposure to MFN and negotiation was not researched, and PDT revenue was -1.5% at constant rates in H1 FY2026, so lower prices do not make the cost case stronger by themselves [CLM-005064].
- **BET-02 in SCN-5: robust to neutral.** The baseline and shadow-mode tranche survives; the USD 350m roll-out is the first large item to be deferred when leverage binds.
- **BET-03 in SCN-3: robust to neutral.** Payoff is 2035-2038, after the Entyvio erosion window; it does not cushion SCN-3 and competes for the same cash.
- **BET-04 in SCN-3: robust to neutral.** Needed in every scenario, but the Medicare price from January 2028 is statutory and PBM private-label biosimilars route around contracts [CLM-007026] [CLM-007015]. Value holds only as delay.
- **BET-05 in SCN-5: neutral to fragile.** SCN-5 contains launch slippage; PsA binary readout and inspection risk sit directly on this bet [CLM-005037] [CLM-012134].
- **BET-06 in SCN-2: robust to neutral.** Option value does not depend on AI pace; 'robust' overstates it when no option has produced an approval [CLM-009097].
- **BET-06 in SCN-3: robust to neutral.** The loss is capped, but options pay after 2035 and do not defend Entyvio net price in 2028-2034; 'robust' should mean value holds, not that the loss is small.
- **BET-06 in SCN-5: robust to fragile.** Upfronts and option fees are cash (ASM-106 excludes only contingent milestones); the constrained portfolio still allocates USD 300m (19%) here, and it is the first line to cut.
- **BET-07 in SCN-1: robust to option_value.** If AI accelerates, models commoditise (the SCN-1 driver) and value accrues to partners and to owners of proprietary data, not to licensees [CLM-006056].
- **BET-09 in SCN-3: robust to neutral.** A rented-assay service is irrelevant to price pressure; its value is cost avoidance against an unknown baseline (IVB-002).
- **BET-11 in SCN-3: robust to fragile.** SC pens move patients into the pharmacy benefit where PBM biosimilar templates operate [CLM-007015] [CLM-007016]; the effect after entry is unestablished [CLM-007053].
- **BET-11 in SCN-5: robust to neutral.** Device capex is deferrable; the existing pen and HYHUB stay.
- **BET-12 in SCN-2: robust to neutral.** The pathway hypothesis has no outcome evidence in any scenario [CLM-007039]; 'robust' should be reserved for bets with evidence of value.
- **BET-12 in SCN-3: robust to neutral.** As above; compliance exposure rises under pricing pressure [CLM-007010].
- **BET-12 in SCN-4: robust to option_value.** Care-model change could make pathway data valuable; no evidence yet [CLM-007045].
- **BET-13 in SCN-4: robust to option_value.** Unproven; Flatiron's neutrality does not transfer [CLM-011050].
- **BET-15 in SCN-2: robust to neutral.** Need for a bridge does not depend on AI pace.
- **BET-15 in SCN-3: option_value to fragile.** Under pricing pressure free cash flow falls while leverage already fails the 3.0x test by council arithmetic [CLM-005005] [CLM-005014].

## 3. Which bets break in each scenario

'Breaks' means rated fragile by the council: value falls or spend is stranded. Stranded capital is the sum of the balanced totals of those bets (a ceiling, because gates would stop spend before the full total is committed).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SCN-1 | BET-16 | 0 | 0% | BET-01, BET-02, BET-03, BET-08, BET-09, BET-17 | 1380 |
| SCN-2 | BET-07, BET-10, BET-14, BET-16 | 180 | 6% | BET-01, BET-02, BET-04, BET-05, BET-08, BET-09, BET-11, BET-17 | 1390 |
| SCN-3 | BET-10, BET-11, BET-14, BET-15, BET-16 | 190 | 6% |  | 0 |
| SCN-4 | BET-14 | 0 | 0% | BET-01, BET-06 | 900 |
| SCN-5 | BET-01, BET-03, BET-05, BET-06, BET-10, BET-13, BET-14, BET-15, BET-16 | 1660 | 56% | BET-04, BET-08, BET-09, BET-17 | 390 |

Reading *(council interpretation)*:

- **SCN-1 (AI accelerates).** Nothing funded breaks. The risk is the opposite: BET-07 and BET-08 look like winners but the model commoditisation driver means Takeda gains only where it owns proprietary data (BET-01, BET-09). The bets that would pay (BET-07 at USD 180m) are option-sized, so the portfolio under-invests relative to an SCN-1 world by design, and the ambitious portfolio (USD 4,975m) is the only one that scales on it. Trigger: Nabla first-in-human and an independent Lilly-lab result [CLM-006039] [CLM-000281].
- **SCN-2 (AI incremental).** BET-07 breaks (USD 180m, small) and BET-10 and BET-14 would break if funded. BET-01, BET-08, BET-09 and BET-17 survive as productivity and discipline spend. Note the SCN-2 world is the one the registry currently supports: no independent outcome exists for any AI platform [CLM-011052], and AI-origin Phase 2 success is cited as conventional [CLM-006049]. Treat SCN-2 as the base case for AI spending.
- **SCN-3 (pricing, regulatory and biosimilar pressure).** The most consequential scenario for the portfolio and the one the strategy treats most generously: it rates 7 bets robust, the council rates 0. BET-11 breaks (pharmacy-benefit channel), BET-15 breaks (leverage), and BET-04 is needed but insufficient because the January 2028 Medicare price is statutory. The exposure the portfolio is built to cover (JPY 383-766bn a year of Entyvio revenue at ASM-024) is addressed by no bet except through delay, so SCN-3 is where the strategy's gap is largest.
- **SCN-4 (new modalities, care models, tech-native entrants).** Few bets break because few depend on it. BET-07 holds option value, but tech-native entrants (Isomorphic reportedly raised USD 600m [CLM-011014]) win partnerships that Takeda's USD 180m cannot match; BET-06 is the only bet that buys modality exposure and its payoff comes after 2035.
- **SCN-5 (combined downside).** BET-01, BET-03, BET-05, BET-06 and BET-13 break (BET-15 by arithmetic). See section 4.

## 5. Stress cases the strategy does not model (council-added, not in scenarios.json)

**S-A. Early Entyvio biosimilar entry (2028-2029) with an Amitiza outcome at or above the provision.** ASM-025 (2031, range 2030-2032) excludes this. Basis: two biosimilar BLAs are under FDA review with one decision expected Q1 2027 per trade reporting, the reference product was approved in 2014, and Takeda says earlier launches must address patent validity and infringement [CLM-014051] [CLM-014050] [CLM-005017]. Which bets break:

| Bet | Effect under S-A |
|---|---|
| BET-04 | Playbook RM-026 (2029 H2) is dated after the earliest assumed entry (2030). It would be late. Re-date to 2028 H1 at no extra capital. |
| BET-05 | Becomes the only replacement in time; PsA readout (May 2027) and launch coverage carry more weight than their USD 180m suggests. |
| BET-03 | Payoff 2035-2038 misses the window entirely. |
| BET-06 | First approval 2035 (RM-043) misses the window; capital competes with the bridge. |
| BET-11 | SC migration before entry would increase pharmacy-benefit exposure at the worst moment. |
| BET-15 | A bridge purchase is needed sooner and is infeasible at 3.13x to 3.41x. This is where DEFER becomes a decision to leave a gap. |

**S-B. AI-design success with no data advantage (SCN-1 in which Takeda is a licensee).** Partners and AI-native biotechs capture the value (SCN-1 driver: commodity models). BET-07 pays only success-based milestones, which are contingent liabilities above USD 1bn per deal [CLM-006039] [CLM-006041]. Takeda's gain depends on BET-01 and BET-09, which are small. The portfolio is not harmed but does not benefit.

**S-C. Launch-wave concentration.** Three launches within 12 months [CLM-005074] with US revenue already falling 9.0% at actual rates in FY2025 [CLM-005001]. BET-04, BET-11 and BET-12 sit in the same US organisation (stage-gate owners). A launch problem in that organisation breaks three bets together, which the scenario table treats as independent.

## 6. Robust core (least-regret set)

The core is the least-regret set: bets the council would keep funded in every scenario and in S-A, either because value holds or because the bet addresses a dated or event-driven exposure at a capped cost. BET-03 and BET-05 are rated fragile in SCN-5 yet stay in the core as floors: BET-05 is the only replacement-revenue bet acting inside the Entyvio window and BET-03 is the only in-house diversification, so their fragility is the risk being managed, not a reason to cut. Council view, balanced H1 2027-2029 tranche (strategy's initial figures, ASSUMPTION):

| Bet | Core content | H1 USD m |
|---|---|---|
| BET-04 Entyvio franchise defence: payer contracting, evidence and lifecycle | Fixed dates, cheapest against exposure; re-date the playbook to 2028. | 40 |
| BET-05 Zasocitinib breadth and immunology successor evidence | Existing asset; the evidence is the best in the set. | 60 |
| BET-11 Drug-device and home/SC administration platform | Existing capability; hold device capex to a baseline-first tranche (condition: resolve the PBM-channel conflict with BET-04). | 50 |
| BET-17 AI use-case measurement, decision support and reskilling | Cheap discipline that makes the AI claims testable; move measurement ownership away from the savings owner. | 40 |
| BET-01 Governed cross-franchise R&D data and decision layer | Thin slice only (metadata standard and decision log) until gate 3. | 60 |
| BET-09 Rented automated data generation (CRO and cloud labs) with ladder triggers | Rented, reversible; stage 1-2 only. | 25 |
| BET-08 Federated data consortium participation | Near-free and measurable. | 10 |
| BET-02 Plasma process-intelligence and fixed-price automation programme | Baseline and shadow-mode tranche only (not the roll-out). | 80 |
| BET-03 Orexin science as a multi-asset discovery platform | Floor of discovery spend with a Phase 1 safety gate before H2 (constrained size). | 100 |
| **Core total** | | **465** |

The balanced H1 tranche is USD 685m. The core is USD 465m (68%). The remaining USD 220m (BET-06 150, BET-07 40, BET-12 30) should be released only on triggers: a deal-outcome scorecard (RM-007) for BET-06, Nabla first-in-human data for BET-07 beyond existing commitments, and an identified pathway bottleneck for BET-12.

**What the core does not do.** It does not replace Entyvio revenue. It protects what is dated and measurable, keeps learning cheap and holds options open. The Entyvio gap is covered only by the existing pipeline, BET-05 breadth and, if built, a mid-size licensing channel that no bet funds (see decision memo).

## 7. Capital timing against the Entyvio window

Bets the strategy's own evaluation puts at eight or more years to payoff are BET-03 (10), BET-06 (10), BET-07 (8) and BET-13 (8). Their balanced totals are USD 1460m, 49.5% of USD 2,950m. Their payoff years (2035-2038) fall after the Entyvio erosion window that ASM-025 places at 2031-2034. The bets that act inside the window (BET-04, BET-05, BET-11) total USD 510m, 17% of the balanced envelope.

## 8. Method limits

- Ratings are interpretation. Takeda internal data (IVB-004, IVB-005, IVB-006, IVB-012) could change them.
- Stranded-capital sums are ceilings. Stage gates cap capital at risk per gate (ASM-037 convention).
- Scenario 'robust' in the strategy table means value holds or rises; the council applied the same definition and withheld 'robust' where the bet's value rests on a hypothesis with no outcome evidence.
