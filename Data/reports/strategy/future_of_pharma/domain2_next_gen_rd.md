# Domain 2: Next-generation R&D (A11b, wave 3)

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Takeda FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026. Options are labelled EVIDENCE_BACKED or HYPOTHESIS; none is an endorsement. Baseline classes are from [capability_baseline.md](../../New_mediciine/companies/takeda_public_baseline/capability_baseline/capability_baseline.md).

Problem addressed: replace revenue lost to patent expiry with new molecules faster and with a higher probability of success, using new modalities and computational design. *(INTERPRETATION)*

## What the evidence shows
**AI-enabled discovery: deployed versus announced**
- Lilly: Isomorphic collaboration (USD 45 million upfront, up to USD 1.7 billion milestones, 2024-01-07) is at concept stage [CLM-006012]; the Chai custom antibody model is a trade report and Chai's hit-rate claim is Chai's own [CLM-006010]; no candidate is named in any opened source [CLM-006007].
- AbbVie: BigHat (USD 30 million upfront) at prototype and Valkai at concept [CLM-006035, CLM-006037].
- Takeda: Nabla Bio second collaboration (October 2025; double-digit millions upfront, over USD 1 billion success-based) with first-in-human data expected in one to two years, and Iambic (February 2026, success-based payments could exceed USD 1.7 billion) [CLM-006039, CLM-006041]. Nabla states a three to four week design-to-test loop, a company claim [CLM-006040]. All are prototype or earlier [CLM-006045].
- Outcome evidence from the wider industry is limited and mixed: Recursion ended four of 11 programmes in 2025 [CLM-006047]; Insilico's rentosertib Phase IIa (71 patients) showed +98.4 mL FVC at 60 mg with seven discontinuations for liver injury [CLM-006048]; AI-origin Phase 2 success is cited as about the same as conventional (secondary citation) [CLM-006049]. Synthesis: [CLM-011057].

**New modalities: acquired or optioned, not built**
- AbbVie: Capstan (in vivo CAR-T, USD 2.1 billion) and the ADARx siRNA option (USD 335 million upfront, up to USD 7.5 billion milestones) [CLM-009058, CLM-009061]. Lilly: Verve (gene editing) and POINT (radioligands) [CLM-009010, CLM-009006]. J&J: Ambrx (ADC) and Halda [CLM-009035, CLM-009032]. Maturity: early clinical or concept; outcomes not yet visible [CLM-011059].

**Takeda's route to date**
- Window approvals came from conventional routes: oveporexton from in-house orexin science (approved 2026-08-05), rusfertide from Protagonist (approved 2026-08-28), zasocitinib via the Nimbus purchase (NDA planned 2026) [CLM-012149, CLM-012151, CLM-009076, CLM-009077]. Takeda exited cell therapy in October 2025, recognising a JPY 58.2bn impairment (JPY 58.0bn was the earlier expectation) [CLM-005025, CLM-009084]. Synthesis: [CLM-011058].
- Takeda's stated peak potential for six late-stage programs is USD 10-20bn [CLM-005041]; early pipeline depth beyond them is a gap (IVB-008) [CLM-005044].

**Contrary evidence**
- Platform pipelines still suffer ordinary attrition [CLM-006047]; Lilly's DICE and AbbVie's Cerevel purchases ended in discontinuation or large impairment [CLM-009003, CLM-009054], so acquiring modalities is not safer than building them.

## Where proprietary advantage exists
- Disease biology depth (orexin) and clinical execution are proprietary and not purchasable quickly [CLM-005048, CLM-005042].
- Generative models and structure tools are converging on open or shared models (OpenFold3, Boltz, Chai), so the model itself is a weaker moat than proprietary assay and clinical data [CLM-006056].
- Partnering is better for new modalities where Takeda lacks platform scale; options reduce capital at risk compared with asset purchases [CLM-009099, CLM-009061] *(INTERPRETATION)*.

## Success criteria
- First-in-human data from Nabla-designed molecules within the stated one to two years, with candidate quality compared against conventional molecules [CLM-006039] *(HYPOTHESIS)*.
- Share of Phase 1 starts originating in AI or option programmes by FY2028 and their Phase 2 transition rate versus the conventional rate (target to be set internally) *(HYPOTHESIS)*.
- Late-stage delivery to the announced timetable continues (three Phase 3 readouts, then filings in FY2025-FY2029) [CLM-005042].

## Risks
- Spending on AI deals with success-based payments above USD 1 billion each, which are contingent liabilities rather than demonstrated value [CLM-006039, CLM-006041].
- Impairments after failed acquisitions (Takeda JPY 58.2bn cell therapy, JPY 27.8bn Maverick, JPY 21.5bn soticlestat) show portfolio stop decisions have a cash cost [CLM-009083, CLM-009084].
- FDA draft AI guidance is non-binding but sets expectations for model credibility in regulatory submissions [CLM-006046].
