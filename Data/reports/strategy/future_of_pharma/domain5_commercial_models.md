# Domain 5: Commercial models (A11b, wave 3)

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Takeda FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026. Options are labelled EVIDENCE_BACKED or HYPOTHESIS; none is an endorsement. Evidence draws on A7's [commercial_models_comparison.md](../../New_mediciine/industry_transformation/commercial_patient/commercial_models_comparison.md) and [transfer_to_takeda.md](../../New_mediciine/industry_transformation/commercial_patient/transfer_to_takeda.md).

Problem addressed: protect and grow net revenue as payers, PBMs and price negotiation compress margins, and launch new brands on time. *(INTERPRETATION)*

Design rule for this domain: separate **decision support** (AI that advises people) from **automation that creates compliance risk** (AI that generates, sends or targets promotional or patient-facing content without review). Evidence below is assigned to one or the other.

## What the evidence shows
**Payer, PBM and policy dynamics (largest effect on commercial models)**
- CVS Caremark preferred Wegovy over Zepbound from 1 July 2025 (Lilly stock fell over 11% on the announcement) and restored Zepbound as an additional preferred option from 1 October 2026 [CLM-007011, CLM-007013].
- From 1 July 2026 CVS template formularies prefer interchangeable Stelara biosimilars [CLM-007015]. AbbVie reports USD 65.1bn of rebate and chargeback provisions in 2025 [CLM-007019]. AbbVie delayed Humira erosion by rebate-for-access contracting and a co-branded supply route [CLM-003085].
- Takeda: Entyvio was selected for Medicare price negotiation effective January 2028 [CLM-007026]. Synthesis: [CLM-011071].

**Launch execution**
- Mounjaro USD 22,965m and Zepbound USD 13,542m in 2025, 56% of Lilly revenue, with lower realised prices [CLM-007031, CLM-007024]. Skyrizi USD 17,562m and Rinvoq USD 8,304m [CLM-007020]. Carvykti USD 1,887m was capacity-limited; Spravato USD 1,696m was site-limited [CLM-007029].
- Takeda: Entyvio JPY 958.0bn (21% of revenue) and QDENGA JPY 40.8bn; oveporexton and zasocitinib launches are pending [CLM-007027, CLM-005045, CLM-007033]. New-brand launch execution is POTENTIAL_NEW_CAPABILITY [CLM-005069, CLM-005074].

**AI in commercial work: deployed versus announced**
- J&J reports AskJIA (sales assistant), Engagement.AI (provider insights, 14 countries) and a quality-control answer tool, with no usage or outcome data [CLM-006023, CLM-006021]. Maturity: operational per executive, self-reported.

**Contrary evidence**
- Cash-pay channels are limited by copay accumulators and anti-kickback rules; the self-pay share of Zepbound revenue is not in the 10-K [CLM-007010, CLM-007005]. Whether TrumpRx volumes are material is unknown [CLM-007009].

## Where proprietary advantage exists
- Contracting scale and payer relationships (AbbVie Humira, Skyrizi/Rinvoq) and launch infrastructure are slow to copy but removable by a PBM template change [CLM-011086, CLM-007015].
- Clinical differentiation drives pull; commercial AI tools are commodity and replicable *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-006023].
- Open standards or partnering suit data-sharing with payers and health systems, and specialty distribution *(HYPOTHESIS)*.

## Success criteria
- Entyvio net price and formulary position retained at target after 2028 negotiation and any biosimilar entry *(HYPOTHESIS)*.
- Launch metrics for oveporexton and zasocitinib: time to first prescription, site activation rate, payer coverage at 6 and 12 months *(HYPOTHESIS)*.
- For decision-support pilots: hours saved or pre-call quality score against a control group *(HYPOTHESIS)*.

## Risks
- Compliance: promotional content and patient communication are regulated; FDA draft guidance treats AI credibility as risk-based [CLM-006046]. Keep humans accountable for outbound content *(INTERPRETATION)*.
- Policy: Part D redesign and IRA selection affect profitability [CLM-007026].
- Dependence on a single PBM decision (Zepbound swing in either direction) [CLM-007011, CLM-007013].

## Evidence and gaps
Gaps: net price per prescription except AbbVie provisions, Takeda patient-assistance and field-force detail, self-pay share of Zepbound revenue [CLM-007005, CLM-007058]. See [evidence_gaps.md](evidence_gaps.md).
