# Domain 4: Care models (A11b, wave 3)

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Takeda FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026. Options are labelled EVIDENCE_BACKED or HYPOTHESIS; none is an endorsement. Evidence draws on A7's [commercial_patient](../../New_mediciine/industry_transformation/commercial_patient/) reports.

Problem addressed: time to therapy, adherence and site-of-care cost for chronic, rare and plasma-derived treatments. *(INTERPRETATION)*

## What the evidence shows
**Deployed versus announced**
- Lilly: LillyDirect self-pay (Zepbound vials from August 2024 at USD 399/549, cut to USD 299/399/449 on 1 December 2025; KwikPen from USD 299); Lilly says over 1 million patients used LillyDirect in 2025 and one in three new branded weight-management starts was Zepbound self-pay vials (company-reported, footnotes unverified) [CLM-007000, CLM-007002, CLM-007044, CLM-007004].
- J&J: OTTAVA robot has De Novo authorisation (FDA decision 2026-07-21; announced 2026-07-22) and one announced installation, pilot maturity [CLM-000283, CLM-006029]; the A10 audit notes the release says the hospital "will be the first to install", so completion is not shown [CLM-000284]. Polyphonic is early access to select hospitals [CLM-006030].
- AbbVie Allē (over 6 million members, Jan 2024) is a loyalty scheme for aesthetics [CLM-007036]. Takeda Help at Hand: over 100,000 patients cumulative [CLM-007037].
- Convenience shifts: Entyvio subcutaneous pen approved in US, Europe, Japan and cited by Takeda as driving uptake; HYHUB needle-free transfer for Hyqvia received 510(k) clearance in July 2025 (clearance, not adoption) [CLM-007041, CLM-007042]. Counter-example: Spravato is restricted to certified sites under observation [CLM-007030].
- No adherence, persistence or time-to-therapy outcome was found for any company's programme; reported figures are enrolment or access counts [CLM-007039]. Synthesis: [CLM-011067].

**Contrary evidence**
- Pear Therapeutics, a prescription digital therapeutics company, filed for Chapter 11 on 7 April 2023 after payers could deny payment; commentary says evidence was strong but reimbursement stalled [CLM-007045, CLM-007046]. Decentralised-trial disclosures were not usable in the Lilly or J&J 10-Ks [CLM-007048].

## Where proprietary advantage exists
- Drug-device integration and formulation (an SC pen, a needle-free transfer device) is proprietary and tied to the product and its patents [CLM-007041, CLM-007042].
- Patient-services hubs are replicable by vendors; advantage would come from measured outcomes, which no company publishes *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-007039].
- Open standards and partnering suit diagnosis pathways, site networks and data sharing with health systems (Polyphonic-type networks are still pilots) [CLM-006030].

## Success criteria
- Time from prescription to first dose and persistence at 12 months by channel, for oveporexton and Entyvio SC *(HYPOTHESIS)*.
- Share of Entyvio patients on SC and of Hyqvia patients on device-assisted home use *(HYPOTHESIS)*.

## Risks
- Compliance: patient-support and cash-pay programmes interact with anti-kickback and copay accumulator rules [CLM-007010].
- Reimbursement risk for anything sold as a separate digital product [CLM-007045].
- Safety: restricted-distribution or home-use designs need risk management plans (Spravato REMS shows the model) [CLM-007030].

## Evidence and gaps
Gaps: Takeda hub and patient-assistance inventory (IVB-013), Entyvio IV/SC mix, RWE outputs for tirzepatide and Entyvio not retrieved, connected-device features not found [CLM-007047]. See [evidence_gaps.md](evidence_gaps.md).
