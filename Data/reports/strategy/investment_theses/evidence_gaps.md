- Option outcomes: none from the window had produced an approved product by cutoff [CLM-009097].
- Antitrust and policy review of large transactions was not researched [CLM-009098].
- Devices and care models: no adherence, persistence or time-to-therapy outcome for any company [CLM-007039].

## Method limits of this module

- PTS and commercial probability ranges are judgment (ASM-020, ASM-021, ASM-022, ASM-028), not derived from Takeda data.
- Scenario robustness is an interpretation of bet design and competitor evidence.
- Stage-gate capital is a convention (ASM-037); owner roles are generic and delegated limits are an ASSUMPTION.
- No registry or shard writes were made; new evidence needs would be added under A11_strategy ids from 011500.
