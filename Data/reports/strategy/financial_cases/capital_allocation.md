**Why no NPV is reported.** A risk-adjusted NPV needs Takeda's own revenue forecast, probability of success and net price, none of which is public (IVB-004, IVB-005). The company's USD 10-20bn peak estimate for six late-stage programmes is stated not to be probability-adjusted and not a forecast [CLM-005041] [CLM-014049]. Reporting an NPV from public data would convert an assumption into a number that looks like a result. (RECOMMENDATION: compute rNPV internally for BET-05, BET-06 and BET-18 named assets only.)

**Illustrative exposure arithmetic (SCENARIO, not forecast).** Entyvio FY2025 revenue JPY 958.0bn [CLM-005009]. At ASM-024 (40-80% erosion over three years after first entry) annual revenue at risk is JPY 383-766bn, about 8.5-17.0% of FY2025 revenue (JPY 4,505.7bn [CLM-005002]). The balanced strategic-bet envelope of USD 1,920m is about JPY 288bn at ASM-001 over fourteen years; BET-04 is JPY 21bn of that. The comparison shows scale, not payback.

## Real-option logic *(INTERPRETATION)*

Options are held where the loss is capped and a signal arrives within the plan: BET-06 and BET-07 (upfronts only, contingent payments cost nothing without data [CLM-006039] [CLM-009099]), BET-09 stage-3 triggers [CLM-011066], BET-13 (unfunded) and BET-18 (contingent on the leverage test). The cost of holding an option is time: the Lilly-NVIDIA lab and Nabla data arrive 2026-2028, so the first decision point on AI scale is 2028 H2.

## Sensitivities that move the allocation

1. Entyvio entry year and erosion (ASM-024, ASM-025): earlier or deeper erosion raises the case for BET-05 and BET-18 and shortens the time available for BET-03.
2. Plasma headroom (ASM-031, IVB-006): below threshold, BET-02's follow-on moves to other core uses; above, the ambitious portfolio's plasma line is justified.
3. Amitiza cash and leverage (ASM-033, ASM-034, IVB-012): drives the constrained versus balanced choice.
4. AI evidence (SCN-1 versus SCN-2): decides whether BET-01, BET-07 and BET-09 scale.

## What is deliberately not allocated

A large asset purchase (BET-15, rejected as framed; the bridge is BET-18), a TuneLab-style platform (BET-14), an owned autonomous lab (BET-10) and a U.S. cash-pay channel (BET-16). Reasons are in [strategic_bets.md](../investment_theses/strategic_bets.md).
