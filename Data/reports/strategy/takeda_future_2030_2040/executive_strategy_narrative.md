# Executive strategy narrative: Takeda 2027-2040 (A11c v2, wave 6)

**Version v2** (A11c-bis, 2026-11-21): numbers and decisions follow the Director's decision record. See 'Changes from v1' at the end.

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. This narrative summarises the proposals in [strategic_bets.md](../investment_theses/strategic_bets.md), [capital_allocation.md](../financial_cases/capital_allocation.md), [stage_gates.md](../financial_cases/stage_gates.md), [scenarios_2027_2040.md](../scenarios/scenarios_2027_2040.md), [loe_resilience_strategy.md](../pipeline_resilience/loe_resilience_strategy.md) and [roadmap_2027_2040.md](../strategic_roadmap/roadmap_2027_2040.md). It uses public evidence only. Takeda's budget and internal data are unknown, so every figure that is not a registry claim is an ASSUMPTION with an id. FACT, INTERPRETATION, HYPOTHESIS, SCENARIO and RECOMMENDATION are kept apart.

## 1. What is changing

*(FACT)* Entyvio, 21% of FY2025 revenue (JPY 958.0bn), loses regulatory exclusivity in the latter half of the decade, has patents expiring in 2032, faces two biosimilar BLAs under FDA review and enters Medicare negotiation in January 2028 [CLM-005009] [CLM-005017] [CLM-014051] [CLM-007026]. v2 widens the entry-year range to 2028-2032 (central case 2031 unchanged) and adds an early-entry stress case (ASM-025, ASM-038). PBM template formularies now decide biosimilar outcomes [CLM-007015]. AI and automation are being built at scale by competitors, but no company in the set shows an independent outcome [CLM-011052].

## 2. Who is winning, and why

*(FACT)* Lilly's revenue rose from USD 28.3bn to USD 65.2bn between FY2021 and FY2025 because tirzepatide reached 56.0% of sales, backed by capacity spending [CLM-000133] [CLM-001003] [CLM-000172]. AbbVie replaced Humira with Skyrizi and Rinvoq because the successors were already marketed across Humira's indications [CLM-003014] [CLM-003084]. J&J absorbed Stelara with a diversified base; its largest brand is 15.2% of revenue [CLM-002073]. *(INTERPRETATION)* Winning here means pre-built successors, capacity and diversification. It does not yet mean AI: Lilly's advantage in growth is a launched molecule in a supply-short category, and realised prices already fell 15% in Q3 2026 [CLM-014003]. The ranking of 'new-molecule revenue share' is not used because the audit marks it invalid as a productivity comparison.

## 3. What is defensible

*(INTERPRETATION)* Compute and third-party models are purchasable; proprietary data, capacity and clinical execution are not [CLM-006055] [CLM-011085]. Competitor data, learning and ecosystem moats have no independent outcome evidence; infrastructure, IP and workflow moats are the best evidenced but each is removable (announced capacity is not operating capacity, patents expire, PBM templates change) [CLM-006054] [CLM-006001] [CLM-007015].

## 4. What Takeda already has

*(FACT)* A plasma network of 270+ centres with adaptive nomograms and a USD 230m fractionation expansion; in-house orexin science that produced an approval on 2026-08-05; late-stage execution on schedule (three approvals or filings); stop discipline (soticlestat, cell therapy exit with a JPY 58.2bn impairment); and deal flow with Nimbus, Protagonist and Innovent [CLM-005060] [CLM-005065] [CLM-005048] [CLM-012149] [CLM-005042] [CLM-005024] [CLM-005025] [CLM-009076] [CLM-009078]. The enterprise data platform exists but outcomes are unproven; lab automation, AI-enabled discovery and patient services are public-evidence gaps, not proven absences [CLM-005058] [CLM-005067] [CLM-005049] [CLM-005072] [CLM-005080].

## 5. Where the opportunity is

*(RECOMMENDATION)* In order of evidence strength: (1) defend and extend the dated Entyvio exposure (BET-04, BET-11); (2) back the replacement set already in hand (BET-05) and hold a funded, contingent mid-size licensing channel for the 2031-2034 gap (BET-18); (3) test whether plasma process data (BET-02, EXPERIMENT: baseline and shadow-mode first, because no plasma peer is benchmarked) and orexin biology (BET-03, at the constrained size with a Phase 1 gate) can become compounding assets; (4) hold cheap options on AI and new modalities and let data decide (BET-06, BET-07, BET-08, BET-09); (5) build the measurement discipline that makes every AI claim testable (BET-01, BET-17, BET-12).

## 6. What to build, what to partner, what to reject

BUILD: governed data and decision layer on the existing platform (BET-01), orexin platform at USD 300m (BET-03), Entyvio defence with a benefit-channel gate (BET-04), devices, baseline first (BET-11). INVEST: zasocitinib breadth (BET-05). PARTNER: scorecard-first options and licences (BET-06), milestone-gated AI design (BET-07), federated consortia (BET-08), rented automation (BET-09), Phase 2/3 licensing channel, contingent (BET-18, new). EXPERIMENT: plasma process intelligence, baseline and shadow-mode (BET-02, was BUILD), launch pathways at USD 50m (BET-12), AI measurement and decision support (BET-17, owner independent of the CFO). DEFER: neutral registry (BET-13, no carry). REJECT: owned autonomous lab (BET-10, with an operational trigger set), TuneLab-style platform (BET-14), a USD 5bn+ purchase (BET-15, was DEFER), U.S. cash-pay channel (BET-16). Sequencing: the data standard and rented automation start together in H1 2027; stage-3 closed-loop work waits for the full data layer and three observable triggers [CLM-011066].

## 7. What to invest

*(ASSUMPTION)* Three portfolios of incremental capital over 2027-2040: capital-constrained USD 1,130m (v1 1,590m), balanced USD 1,920m committed at gates (v1 2,950m), ambitious USD 3,155m (v1 4,975m) (ASM-003, ASM-002, ASM-004), roughly 1.8%, 3.0% and 5.0% of fourteen years of flat R&D at ASM-001. Balanced is the proposed starting point: the H1 2027-29 tranche is USD 595m, of which USD 465m is core (BET-01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 08, 09, 11, 17) and USD 130m (BET-06, 07, 12) is released only on triggers. About USD 1,030m of contingent headroom is reserved for the BET-18 licensing channel (ASM-118); it is not a saving and is released only if the leverage test passes (ASM-033, ASM-119, IVB-012). A USD 5bn+ purchase is rejected as framed.

## 8. How to execute

Stage gates with stop and expansion criteria and named roles: discovery, feasibility, prototype, pilot, scale, value review. The first decision points are the Entyvio benefit-channel gate (2027 H1), the plasma-peer and additionality gates (2027 H2), the January 2028 Entyvio posture with the biosimilar playbook live in 2028 H1, and the Nabla and Iambic go/no-go (2028 H2). A portfolio review each year compares scenario signals (SCN-1 to SCN-5) and reallocates between the three portfolios.

## 9. How to measure

Baselines before claims: AI use cases with owner, baseline and measured benefit; yield per litre and cost per litre at the plasma baseline and shadow-mode run, with a demand-versus-capacity test; time to first dose and persistence for launches; Entyvio net price and persistence by payer segment; option conversion and impairment share; revenue concentration against the Entyvio-era 21% [CLM-005009]. Usage and training counts are not value [CLM-006022] [CLM-014043].

## 10. Reader paragraphs

**CEO.** The decision is whether to treat Entyvio's cliff as a dated defence plus a refill problem, or as a reason to chase a platform story. The evidence supports the first. The proposal funds the dated defence and the replacement set, keeps AI and new modalities as capped options with stop rules, and explicitly rejects four expensive alternatives (owned autonomous lab, external model platform, U.S. cash-pay, and a USD 5bn+ purchase that fails the 3.0x leverage ceiling by arithmetic). For the owned lab the competitor record is weaker than it looks: Lilly's reason for selling is undisclosed and the NVIDIA joint lab reads as re-platforming [CLM-006017] [CLM-000281]; Zymergen failed on a different model [CLM-006051]; U.S. cash-pay has poor structural fit [CLM-007052]. Execution load is high: a new CEO since 24 June 2026, a restructuring and three launches within twelve months [CLM-005073] [CLM-005074].

**CFO.** Capital is proposed as ranges in three envelopes that depend on leverage (2.6x at March 2026) and Amitiza cash (JPY 403.5bn provision, timing unknown) [CLM-005005] [CLM-005014]. Contingent payments in AI and option deals (over USD 1bn on single deals) cost nothing without data and are not in the envelope [CLM-006039] [CLM-006041]. No NPV is reported: it would require internal forecasts and probabilities (IVB-004, IVB-005, IVB-014). The capital-constrained portfolio keeps the dated defences and drops AI-scale and option spend first; the registry carries no capital in any portfolio. Mid-size licences (BET-18) are released only if leverage stays at or below ASM-033 after Amitiza clarity and a write-down test passes, because AbbVie wrote off 54% of Cerevel's net price [CLM-009054]; council arithmetic puts a USD 5bn purchase at about 3.1x and about 3.4x with Amitiza (ASM-119).

**R&D.** Refill after 2030 depends on early depth that is not public (IVB-008). The proposal funds a second orexin-class asset as discovery spend at the constrained size with a Phase 1 gate and an additionality test, scorecard-first option access to one or two adjacent modalities, and AI-design partnerships gated on first-in-human data and comparator benchmarks [CLM-011038] [CLM-011061] [CLM-006039]. Gate CNS assets on Phase 2 quality: neuroscience was the common failure site across three companies [CLM-003054] [CLM-002039] [CLM-012120]. Rent wet-lab automation rather than own it; the owned-lab failures are weaker evidence than a pattern [CLM-006013] [CLM-011065].

**CTO / Chief Digital and Technology Officer.** Extend the existing agent platform with governance, a decision log and baselines; do not build a competing platform [CLM-005058]. The model layer converges on shared models, so the moat is Takeda's own decision-linked data [CLM-006056] [CLM-006055]. Join federated tasks where measured gains exist [CLM-006043]. Document model credibility against the January 2025 FDA draft framework [CLM-006046]. Sequence: data standard and rented automation together; closed-loop work only after the full layer and three triggers.

**Commercial.** Settle the benefit-channel split in 2027 H1, then build payer-contracting and formulary-defence depth for Entyvio before January 2028 and the biosimilar window (possibly as early as 2028); the aim is to delay and measure erosion, since Humira's contracting delayed but did not prevent it [CLM-003007] [CLM-003002]. Extend SC and home administration baseline-first, and settle with the Entyvio team whether moving patients into the pharmacy benefit helps or hurts after entry, which is unproven [CLM-007041] [CLM-007042] [CLM-007053] [CLM-007015]. Attach a USD 50m-capped launch-pathway pilot to oveporexton from 2027 H1, with a named leader, before redesigning patient programmes, since AbbVie's redesign was reversed within months [CLM-003033]. Do not copy a U.S. cash-pay channel [CLM-007052]. Keep AI in field and medical teams advisory only; no automated promotional content.
