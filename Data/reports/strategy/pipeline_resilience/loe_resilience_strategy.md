# LOE-resilience strategy (A11c v2, wave 6)

**Version v2** (A11c-bis, 2026-11-21): revised to the Director's decision record.

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Takeda fiscal years run April-March (FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026). Goal: reduce Takeda's dependence on single products such as Entyvio and make loss of exclusivity survivable. No bet eliminates LOE; each acts on one of four levers. FACT, INTERPRETATION, SCENARIO and RECOMMENDATION are labelled. Dollar and yen figures that are not registry claims carry ASM ids ([assumptions_register.md](../financial_cases/assumptions_register.md)).

| Company | Evidence | Reading |

| Lilly, tirzepatide | 56.0% of FY2025 revenue (15.6% in FY2023); realised prices -15% in Q3 2026 while volume grew; CVS Caremark removed Zepbound for about 15 months (1 July 2025 to restoration on 1 October 2026); tirzepatide patent 2036 [CLM-001003] [CLM-014003] [CLM-007011] [CLM-007013] [CLM-000250] | Counter-example: growth built on one franchise creates new concentration and price exposure before any LOE |
| Regeneron, EYLEA | U.S. net sales -42% in 2025 despite a platform [CLM-011017] | A platform does not protect a single product |

*(INTERPRETATION)* Three lessons: successors must be marketed before the cliff; payer contracting and patent posture delay but do not stop erosion (Humira fell 78.6% anyway); replacing one concentration with another (Skyrizi plus Rinvoq, tirzepatide) is the failure mode to avoid.

## 6. Resilience indicators to track internally *(RECOMMENDATION; thresholds to be set internally, ASSUMPTION)*

- Share of revenue from the largest product (21% at FY2025 [CLM-005009]); reference points only, not targets: J&J's largest brand 15.2% [CLM-002073], Lilly tirzepatide 56.0% [CLM-001003].
- Revenue from launch products as defined from FY2026 ('New Launches' replaces Growth and Launch Products [CLM-005008]).
- Net price and persistence by payer segment for Entyvio (IVB-004).
- Share of the pipeline's peak potential that is probability-adjusted (IVB-005).
- Option conversion and impairment as a share of deal value (ASM-028; AbbVie 54% of Cerevel net price as a reference [CLM-009054]).

## 7. Risks to this strategy

- Replacement slips: inspection findings, not clinical data, delayed approvals at Lilly and AbbVie [CLM-012134] [CLM-012112]; Takeda has a prior warning letter [CLM-005020].
- Concentration moves: three launches within twelve months [CLM-005074] and four of six late-stage programmes externally sourced create a new dependence on few assets.
- Capital: leverage 2.6x and Amitiza cash timing limit bridge purchases to roughly the ASM-119 headroom (ASM-033, ASM-034, IVB-012).
- Evidence: erosion curve, net price and probability of success are not public (IVB-004, IVB-005).
