# Partnership models: build, partner, license, acquire or invest (A11b, wave 3)

Research cutoff 2026-11-21. Takeda FY2025 = April 2025 to March 2026. For ten counterpart types this file gives the competitor precedent and outcome evidence (A9, A6), Takeda's observed pattern (A5 baseline), and a suggested mode. Modes are options, labelled EVIDENCE_BACKED (follows from outcome evidence) or HYPOTHESIS (reasoned; needs non-public data). They are not endorsements. Deal sources: [deal_inventory.md](../../New_mediciine/industry_transformation/partnerships_ecosystem/deal_inventory.md), [deal_outcomes_scorecard.md](../../New_mediciine/industry_transformation/partnerships_ecosystem/deal_outcomes_scorecard.md), [platform_vs_asset_classification.md](../../New_mediciine/industry_transformation/partnerships_ecosystem/platform_vs_asset_classification.md).

## Reading the modes
- **Build**: develop internally. **Partner**: collaboration without ownership transfer. **License**: pay for rights (upfront, milestones, royalties). **Acquire**: buy the company or asset. **Invest**: minority equity, funds or venture creation.
- Outcome evidence is thin: most AI and automation deals have no disclosed result [CLM-006007, CLM-006045]; value destruction concentrates in neuroscience, gene and cell therapy and robotics [CLM-009100].

## Takeda's observed pattern (baseline)
One flagship acquisition (Nimbus, USD 4.0bn upfront), then licences, options and equity (Innovent, Protagonist, Keros), divestitures, and write-offs of earlier purchases; no significant acquisitions in the three years to March 2026; four of six late-stage programs externally sourced [CLM-011075, CLM-009098, CLM-009087, CLM-005050]. AI-oriented alliances (Nabla, Iambic, BridGene, Charles River, MIT J-Clinic) have no disclosed outcomes [CLM-005049].

## Where the choice of mode changes the economics *(INTERPRETATION)*
- Licence and option payments are mostly contingent: Takeda's AI deals carry over USD 1bn and USD 1.7bn of success-based payments that cost nothing unless data are positive [CLM-006039, CLM-006041]. Acquisitions put cash up front and expose write-down: AbbVie wrote off about 54% of Cerevel's net price [CLM-009054].
- Contingent value rights and sales milestones are used to share risk (Prevail, Akouos, Verve, Abiomed, Nimbus) [CLM-009099].
- A neutral platform (Flatiron) preserves customer base; owner-led platforms (TuneLab) may deter rivals from contributing, but TuneLab and AISB show rivals will join federated models [CLM-011022, CLM-006056].

## Risks
- Counterparty risk: Neurocrine and Denali ended Takeda collaborations [CLM-009090, CLM-009089].
- Evidence risk: sources for several competitor deals are company releases or trade snippets (A9 flags single interested sources) [CLM-009028, CLM-009079].
- Compliance and antitrust: not researched in this module *(gap)*.
