## How to read the table
- A mechanism is a moat only if it is hard for a competitor to replicate and it changes an economic outcome; for data, learning and ecosystem moats the registry holds no independent outcome evidence for any competitor [CLM-006054 as corrected by A10: "wording stronger than sources support"].
- Infrastructure, IP and workflow moats are the best evidenced in competitor financials, but each carries a documented exposure: announced capacity is not operating capacity [CLM-006001]; patents expire (Entyvio) [CLM-005017]; PBM templates change [CLM-007015].
- Takeda's strongest evidenced moats sit in mechanisms 3 (orexin science, in-house), 4 (plasma network) and 7 (late-stage development execution) [CLM-005048, CLM-005063, CLM-005042]. Its weakest evidenced are 1, 2 and 6, where no outcomes are public [CLM-005080].

## Evidence gaps
No audited revenue attributable to any data, learning or ecosystem moat; AbbVie and Lilly venture arms have no investment counts [CLM-009074]; J&J MONARCH and VELYS installed bases are undisclosed [CLM-006026].
