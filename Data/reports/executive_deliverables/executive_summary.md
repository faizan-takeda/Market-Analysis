# MEDICINE 2040: executive summary for Takeda

Cutoff 2026-11-21. Takeda fiscal year (FY) 2025 = April 2025 to March 2026 [CLM-005000]. Takeda amounts stay in JPY. Every dollar figure for a strategic bet is an ASSUMPTION with an ID in the [assumptions register](../strategy/financial_cases/assumptions_register.md); Takeda's own budget is not public (IVB-014).

**Challenge status.** The Strategic Council (119 verdicts: 45 support, 64 conditional, 10 oppose), the red team and the stress test have run. The Director's decisions of 2026-11-21 are applied here as **strategy v2** ([decision record](../strategy/critical_challenges/DIRECTOR_DECISION_RECORD.md)). The v1 strategy files have not yet been rewritten to match, so where they differ this summary says "v2 per Director decision record".

## 1. What is changing

Entyvio was JPY 958.0bn, 21% of FY2025 revenue [CLM-005009]. Takeda says it loses regulatory exclusivity in the latter half of the decade and that some patents expire in 2032 [CLM-005017] [CLM-014050]. Two biosimilar applications are under FDA review [CLM-014051]. CMS selected Entyvio for a negotiated Medicare price from January 2028 [CLM-007026]. CVS template formularies already prefer Stelara biosimilars [CLM-007015]. Vyvanse fell from JPY 423.2bn to 203.2bn in two years [CLM-005015]. Group revenue fell 1.7% (-2.7% at constant rates) in FY2025 [CLM-005001].

*(SCENARIO, not a forecast)* At the ASM-024 erosion range of 40-80% over three years, Entyvio revenue at risk is JPY 383-766bn a year. The base is global while first entry is a U.S. event, so the figure overstates the first three years [CLM-005012] (ASM-024).

## 2. Who is winning

Lilly's revenue rose from USD 28.3bn to USD 65.2bn between FY2021 and FY2025, with tirzepatide at 56.0% of sales [CLM-000133] [CLM-000137] [CLM-001003]. AbbVie replaced Humira (USD 21,237m to 4,540m) with Skyrizi plus Rinvoq (USD 7,687m to 25,866m) [CLM-003002] [CLM-003014]. J&J absorbed a 41.3% Stelara decline with its largest brand at 15.2% of revenue [CLM-000254] [CLM-002073]. Rankings on new-molecule revenue share or approvals per R&D dollar are not used because the audit marks them invalid [CLM-012013] [CLM-000270].

## 3. Why

*(INTERPRETATION)* Winning here means a successor already marketed before the cliff [CLM-003084], capacity built ahead of demand (Lilly capex USD 7,841m in FY2025) [CLM-000172], and a diversified base. It does not yet mean AI: no independent outcome exists for any company's AI programme [CLM-011052] [CLM-006054, wording stronger than its sources support per audit]. Durability is open: Lilly's realised prices fell 15% in Q3 2026 and about USD 900m of that quarter was one-off [CLM-014003].

## 4. What is defensible

*(INTERPRETATION)* Compute and third-party models can be bought; proprietary decision-linked data, built capacity and clinical execution cannot [CLM-006055] [CLM-011085]. Infrastructure, IP and workflow moats are best evidenced and each is removable: announced capacity is not operating capacity, patents expire, PBM templates change [CLM-007015]. *(HYPOTHESIS)* Lilly's data position is the most defensible of the four; the registry has no outcome to confirm it [CLM-000280].

## 5. What Takeda already has

A plasma network of more than 270 centres with adaptive nomograms and a USD 230m Los Angeles fractionation expansion [CLM-005060] [CLM-005065]. In-house orexin science that produced an FDA approval on 2026-08-05 [CLM-005048] [CLM-005029]. Rusfertide approved 2026-08-28 and a zasocitinib NDA accepted under Priority Review [CLM-005032] [CLM-005035]. Three Phase 3 readouts delivered positive on the 2024 plan [CLM-005042]. Stop discipline, including a JPY 58.2bn cell-therapy impairment [CLM-009084]. Deal flow: Nimbus USD 4.0bn upfront, Innovent USD 1.2bn upfront [CLM-009076] [CLM-009078].

Constraints: leverage 2.6x at March 2026, the Amitiza provision of JPY 403.5bn, a CEO in post since 24 June 2026 and three launches in twelve months [CLM-005005] [CLM-005014] [CLM-005073] [CLM-005074]. AI outcomes, lab automation and patient services are public-evidence gaps, not proven absences [CLM-005080] [CLM-005067] [CLM-005072].

## 6. Where the opportunity is

*(RECOMMENDATION)* In order of evidence strength: (1) defend and extend the dated Entyvio exposure (BET-04, BET-11); (2) back the replacement set already in hand (BET-05); (3) compound the two strengths no peer in the set shares, plasma process data and orexin biology (BET-02, BET-03); (4) buy licences for Phase 2/3 assets, the instrument Takeda has actually used (BET-18); (5) hold capped options on AI and new modalities (BET-06 to BET-09); (6) build the measurement discipline that makes AI claims testable (BET-01, BET-17, BET-12).

## 8. What to invest

*(ASSUMPTION; not a Takeda budget)* v1 balanced envelope USD 2,950m (ASM-002) becomes about USD 1,920m committed at gates, plus about USD 1,030m of contingent headroom reserved for BET-18, sized USD 0.5-2bn per deal and released only on IVB-012 (Amitiza cash) and the leverage ceiling ASM-033 (v2 per Director decision record). The core H1 2027-29 tranche is about USD 465m. The headroom is not a saving. Constrained (USD 1,130m, ASM-003) and ambitious (USD 3,155m, ASM-004) are v1 figures pending rescaling. See [investment_roadmap.md](investment_roadmap.md).

*(INTERPRETATION)* BET-15 was rejected because the council's arithmetic puts leverage at 3.13x after a USD 5bn purchase and 3.41x if the Amitiza provision is also debt-funded, against the 3.0x planning ceiling (ASM-033); free cash flow is ignored [CLM-005005] [CLM-005014].

## 9. How to execute

Each bet moves through discovery, feasibility, prototype, pilot, scale and value review, with stop and expansion criteria ([stage_gates.md](../strategy/financial_cases/stage_gates.md)). Dated anchors: Entyvio posture and benefit-channel gate (IVB-004) in 2027 H1; biosimilar-event playbook live 2028 H1 (v2, was 2029 H2); Nabla and Iambic go/no-go 2028 H2 [CLM-006039] [CLM-006041]; plasma scale decision 2029 H2 (RM-025); portfolio review each year against scenario signals SCN-1 to SCN-5.

## 10. How to measure

*(RECOMMENDATION)* Set baselines before claims: AI use cases with owner, baseline and measured benefit (usage and training counts are not value [CLM-014043]); yield and cost per litre at the plasma pilot; time to first dose and persistence for launches; Entyvio net price and persistence by payer segment; option conversion and impairment share; revenue concentration against the Entyvio-era 21% [CLM-005009]. Measurement ownership moves off the CFO, who owns the JPY 200bn savings target [CLM-005059].

## Reader notes

**CEO.** *(RECOMMENDATION)* Treat Entyvio as a dated defence plus a refill problem. The evidence supports that reading over a platform story. Fund the defence and the replacement set, keep AI and new modalities as capped options with stop rules, and decline three expensive alternatives (owned lab, model platform, U.S. cash-pay) where the record shows divestment, distressed sale or poor fit [CLM-006052] [CLM-006051] [CLM-007052]. Execution load is high.

**CFO.** *(ASSUMPTION)* Capital is a range, not a plan. The two binding variables are leverage and Amitiza cash timing (IVB-012). Contingent payments in AI and option deals exceed USD 1bn on single deals and cost nothing without data [CLM-006039] [CLM-006041]. No NPV is reported because it would need internal forecasts (IVB-004, IVB-005). AbbVie wrote off 54% of the Cerevel net price [CLM-009054].

**R&D leader.** *(RECOMMENDATION)* Refill after 2030 depends on early depth that is not public (IVB-008). Fund orexin at USD 300m with a Phase 1 gate and an additionality test against base R&D. Gate CNS assets on Phase 2 quality; neuroscience assets failed at AbbVie, J&J and Takeda [CLM-003054] [CLM-002039] [CLM-012120]. Rent wet-lab automation [CLM-006013].

**CTO.** *(RECOMMENDATION)* Extend the existing agent platform with governance and a decision log; do not build a rival [CLM-005058]. Models converge, so the moat is Takeda's own decision-linked data [CLM-006056] [CLM-006055]. Document model credibility against FDA's January 2025 draft framework [CLM-006046].

**Commercial leader.** *(RECOMMENDATION)* Build payer-contracting depth before January 2028 [CLM-007026]. Humira held at least 97% of volume to March 2024 and still lost 78.6% in three years, so contracting delays erosion rather than preventing it [CLM-003007] [CLM-003002]. Resolve whether subcutaneous conversion helps or hurts after entry (BET-04 and BET-11 conflict) [CLM-007053]. Launch pathways attach to the oveporexton launch. Do not copy a cash-pay channel [CLM-007052].

> **What we could be wrong about**
> - Entyvio entry could come before 2030: widened to 2028-2032 in v2, but no Purple Book or litigation record is in the registry [CLM-014051] (ASM-025).
> - Contracting may not apply: the registry has no Entyvio medical-versus-pharmacy benefit split, and every analogue is a pharmacy-benefit product [CLM-003007] (IVB-004).
> - The council rates no funded bet robust to pricing pressure (SCN-3); the Medicare price is statutory [CLM-007026].
> - Plasma may be demand-bound, not capacity-bound: PDT revenue was -1.5% at constant rates in H1 FY2026 [CLM-005064].
> - Probabilities (ASM-020, 021, 022, 028) are judgment; Takeda cites above 70% for its late-stage set [CLM-014049].
> - Zasocitinib may add concentration, not diversification [CLM-009076] [CLM-012012].
> - 71 of 83 Takeda claims rest on a single interested source, and independent corroboration is recorded on 2 of 1,401 claims [CLM-005033].
