# Recommendations

Cutoff 2026-11-21. Strategy v2: the Director's decisions of 2026-11-21 on the council and red-team challenge are applied ([decision record](../strategy/critical_challenges/DIRECTOR_DECISION_RECORD.md)). All dollar figures are ASSUMPTIONs, not Takeda budgets (IVB-014). Every item below is a *(RECOMMENDATION)* unless it cites a claim.

## 1. What to do first (next 12 months, to 2027 H2)

1. **Close the dated Entyvio questions first (BET-04).** Entyvio is JPY 958.0bn, 21% of revenue, with Medicare negotiation effective January 2028 and two biosimilar applications under FDA review [CLM-005009] [CLM-007026] [CLM-014051]. In 2027 H1 approve erosion scenarios and the patent posture, and run the benefit-channel gate (IVB-004): what share of Entyvio volume sits in the contractable pharmacy channel. The registry has no medical-versus-pharmacy split [CLM-003007]. If most volume sits outside it, redirect part of the USD 140m to provider and site-of-care strategy. Set the target as "delay and measure net price and persistence", not "retain 10 points". Have the biosimilar-event playbook live by 2028 H1 (v2 per Director decision record; was 2029 H2).
2. **Back the replacement set already in hand (BET-05).** Zasocitinib NDA is accepted under Priority Review, and the PsA Phase 3 completes in May 2027 [CLM-005035] [CLM-005037]. Fund breadth and comparative evidence at USD 180m, and measure payer coverage at 6 and 12 months after launch.
3. **Open the Phase 2/3 licensing channel (BET-18, new in v2).** Takeda's approved late-stage set came mostly from licences, and the channel has no funding in v1 [CLM-005050] [CLM-009076] [CLM-009078]. Set a per-deal upfront ceiling of USD 0.5-2bn, tie release to IVB-012 (Amitiza cash) and the 3.0x leverage ceiling (ASM-033), and complete its design template in A11c-bis.
4. **Run the plasma baseline, not the roll-out (BET-02, now EXPERIMENT).** Measure yield, cost per litre and deviations across a sample of centres and Los Angeles (IVB-006). Test whether plasma is demand-bound or capacity-bound: PDT revenue was -1.5% at constant rates in H1 FY2026 [CLM-005064]. Re-base the 2% pass mark (ASM-031) on money before releasing the roughly USD 365m remainder.
5. **Start measurement before value claims (BET-17, BET-01).** Inventory AI and data products with owner and baseline (IVB-001, IVB-003). Adopt a thin-slice data standard and decision log in one franchise and plasma. Move ownership of AI measurement away from the CFO, who owns the JPY 200bn savings target [CLM-005059] [CLM-005078]. Usage counts such as 63.4% workforce use are not value [CLM-014043].
6. **Attach launch pathways to the oveporexton launch (BET-12, capped at USD 50m).** Approval was 2026-08-05; a 2027 H2 pilot would be late [CLM-005029]. Start in 2027 H1 with a named leader.
7. **Rent, do not build, automation (BET-09).** Sign a CRO or cloud-lab pilot with acceptance criteria and a minimal data schema [CLM-006013] [CLM-006016].
8. **Write the stop rules into AI-design contracts before the next payment (BET-07).** First-in-human data from Nabla is expected within one to two years of October 2025; stop or expand in 2028 H2 [CLM-006039]. Wire the benchmark to the BET-01 decision log and the BET-09 cost baseline.
9. **Fund orexin discovery at USD 300m with a Phase 1 gate (BET-03).** Run an additionality test against base R&D and a competitor orexin scan before the H2 tranche [CLM-005048] [CLM-005004].
10. **Build the option engine scorecard first (BET-06, USD 300m).** Release the balance only on the deal-outcome scorecard and the first two deals. No option from the window has yet produced an approved product [CLM-009097].

## 2. What to avoid

- A USD 5bn+ asset purchase (BET-15, REJECT as framed in v2). The council's arithmetic gives 3.13x leverage after the purchase and 3.41x if the Amitiza provision is also debt-funded, against a 3.0x planning ceiling; free cash flow is ignored [CLM-005005] [CLM-005014].
- An owned autonomous lab (BET-10, zero spend). Owned and venture-funded lab models ended in divestment or distressed sale [CLM-006052] [CLM-006051]. Revisit only if three triggers fire: an independent Lilly-lab result, Nabla first-in-human data, a measured CRO cost [CLM-011066] [CLM-000281].
- A TuneLab-style model platform (BET-14). The public record shows no measured Takeda advantage and no external demand [CLM-000280].
- A U.S. cash-pay channel (BET-16). The channel fits infused and specialty-distributed products poorly [CLM-007052].
- Scaling subcutaneous and device spend (BET-11) before baselining it. SC moves patients into the pharmacy benefit, where PBM biosimilar templates operate; persistence after entry is unestablished [CLM-007015] [CLM-007053].
- Redesigning patient programmes without a pilot: AbbVie reversed its loyalty redesign within months [CLM-003033].
- Reading the USD 10-20bn peak range for six late-stage programmes as a forecast; it is not probability-adjusted [CLM-014049].
- Funding the registry (BET-13) now. The v2 carry is removed; a USD 25m options paper is funded from BET-12 in 2029 (RM-028).

## 3. How to decide whether to scale

Decide on the evidence listed at each gate, against a baseline set at the preceding gate, never on spend to date. A missed stop criterion ends or redirects the bet unless the decision authority records a reason in writing. Expansion releases only the next tranche ([stage_gates.md](../strategy/financial_cases/stage_gates.md)).

| Bet | Scale only if | Stop or redirect if |
|---|---|---|
| BET-01 | Measured effect in at least one decision; reuse above the threshold set at feasibility | No attributable benefit in two consecutive annual reviews |
| BET-02 | Shadow-mode predictions beat the nomogram baseline [CLM-005060]; pilot at or above the re-based threshold with clean inspection | No vendor accepts outcome-linked terms; new inspection observations from the pilot [CLM-005020] |
| BET-03 | Clean Phase 1 for a second candidate; Phase 2 design with credible placebo response | Safety or exposure failure; platform claim withdrawn if only one asset by 2040 |
| BET-04 | Pilot retains share; retained share above the scenario midpoint | Retained share below the scenario low case: redirect to lifecycle |
| BET-05 | PsA positive; payer coverage at plan | PsA misses primary endpoint; coverage below plan |
| BET-06 | Conversion at or above 0.10 (ASM-028); fees within band | Conversion below 0.05; impairments above the 54% benchmark [CLM-009054] |
| BET-07 | Data positive and Phase 2 transition at or above conventional | Data absent by 2028 H2 or negative; success-based payments grow faster than data |
| BET-11 | Persistence higher than IV after entry | SC erodes net price faster than it retains patients |
| BET-18 | Leverage at or below ASM-033 after IVB-012; Phase 2 or later data; write-down test passed | Any trigger unmet |

## 4. Measurable success metrics

*(ASSUMPTION targets; Takeda sets the values.)* Metrics and the year each is first read:

- **2028:** inventory of AI and data products with owner, baseline and measured benefit; Entyvio contracting posture and playbook in place (BET-17, BET-01, BET-04).
- **2029:** plasma pilot result against the re-based threshold; AI-partnership decision made on data; checkpoint on the JPY 200bn savings plan with the technology share disclosed internally [CLM-005059].
- **2032:** plasma network status; options exercised; share of Entyvio exposure replaced by labelled indications (BET-02, BET-06, BET-05).
- **2035:** first option-sourced approval check; decisions changed by models with measured effect (BET-06, BET-01).
- **2040:** revenue concentration against the Entyvio-era 21% [CLM-005009].
- **Always:** time to first dose and persistence for launches; Entyvio net price and persistence by payer segment; option conversion and impairment share; cost per data point against the internal baseline. Usage and training counts are not outcomes [CLM-006022] [CLM-014043].
