## 3. Stage gates (stop and expansion criteria)

Each bet moves through discovery, feasibility, prototype, pilot, scale and value review. Rules: decide on the evidence listed, not on spend to date; a missed stop criterion ends or redirects the bet unless the decision authority records a reason in writing; expansion releases the next tranche only; evidence is judged against a baseline set at the preceding gate [CLM-006022]. Owners are roles; delegated limits are an ASSUMPTION. Gate capital is a convention (ASM-037). The table shows the decisive gate per bet; full tables are in [stage_gates.md](../strategy/financial_cases/stage_gates.md).

| Bet | Decisive gate | Stop criterion | Expansion criterion | Owner role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BET-01 | Prototype (2027 H2 gate) | No measured effect on cycle time, hit rate or cost per decision | Measured effect in at least one decision | Chief Digital and Technology Officer |
| BET-02 | Prototype and pilot (2028-29) | Predictions do not beat the nomogram baseline [CLM-005060]; headroom below ASM-031 (2%) | Predictions beat baseline; pilot at or above threshold with clean inspection | Head of Plasma-Derived Therapies |
| BET-03 | Pilot | Safety or exposure failure; weak Phase 2 design | Clean Phase 1; credible Phase 2 design (v2: required before H2 tranche) | Head of Research |
| BET-04 | Feasibility and pilot | No feasible contracting lever; pilot loses share | Levers identified; pilot retains share (v2: IVB-004 benefit-channel gate) | Head of GI franchise and U.S. business unit |
| BET-05 | Pilot | PsA misses primary endpoint [CLM-005037] | PsA positive | Head of R&D |
| BET-06 | Feasibility to value review | Option fees above band; conversion below 0.05 (ASM-028 low) | Within band; conversion at or above 0.10 | Head of Corporate Development |
| BET-07 | Prototype | Data absent by 2028 H2 or negative [CLM-006039] | Data positive against conventional | Head of Research |
| BET-09 | Pilot | Cost above internal baseline | Cost at or below baseline | Head of R&D |
| BET-11 | Pilot | SC erodes net price faster than it retains patients [CLM-007053] | Persistence higher than IV | Head of GI franchise and U.S. business unit |

| BET-17 | Feasibility | No baseline for a use case: stop that use case | Baselines set | v2: an owner independent of the CFO's savings target [CLM-005059] |
| BET-18 | Per deal | Leverage above ASM-033 or IVB-012 unanswered | Both cleared; Phase 2 or later data | Corporate development, Board for large deals |

*(INTERPRETATION)* No funded bet is robust to SCN-3, the scenario that matters most for Entyvio: the council finds the exposure of JPY 383-766bn a year at ASM-024 is addressed by no bet except through delay [CLM-007026] [CLM-007015]. In SCN-5, 850 of the USD 1,130m constrained envelope (53%) sits in bets that break (BET-01, 03, 05, 06), against 19% in the author's table; the stranded balanced capital is USD 1,660m (56%) as a ceiling because gates stop spend earlier. ## 6. Sensitivity: which assumptions move the answer

| Assumption | Value | What changes if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| ASM-024 Entyvio erosion | 60% (range 40-80%); bracketed by Eylea -42% in one year and Humira -78.6% in three [CLM-011017] [CLM-003002] | Deeper or earlier erosion raises the case for BET-05, BET-18 and shortens time for BET-03; no analogue is an infused biologic |
| ASM-025 first entry year | 2031 (range 2030-2032); v2 widens to 2028-2032 | Early entry makes the 2028 H1 playbook the binding date |
| ASM-033, ASM-034 leverage and Amitiza | 3.0x ceiling; JPY 403.5bn (a base case, not a downside; trebling and appeal outcome excluded) [CLM-005014] [CLM-014047] | Decides constrained versus balanced and releases BET-18 |
| ASM-031 plasma pass mark | 2% (range 1-4%), not anchored to money | Without cost per litre the USD 350m follow-on cannot be justified (IVB-006) |
| ASM-020, 021, 022, 028 probabilities | 0.65, 0.15, 0.6, 0.1; judgment | Rankings are only as good as these; Takeda cites above 70% for its late-stage set [CLM-014049] |
| ASM-002 to ASM-004 envelopes | Sums of bet sizes, not a top-down budget | Real envelope could differ by a multiple (IVB-014) |

*(INTERPRETATION)* The three assumptions that most change the portfolio are Entyvio timing and depth (ASM-024, ASM-025), leverage and Amitiza cash (ASM-033, ASM-034), and plasma headroom (ASM-031). No NPV is reported: it would need Takeda's forecasts, net prices and probabilities of success (IVB-004, IVB-005).
