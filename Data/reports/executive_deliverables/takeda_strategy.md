## 2. The seven transformation domains

Each domain states the problem, what competitors show, and Takeda's position. Sources: [strategy/future_of_pharma](../strategy/future_of_pharma/map.md).

**1. Data advantage.** Proprietary data is the one input rivals cannot buy [CLM-006055]. AbbVie built ARCH with fewer than ten full-time staff and opened data from 2,000+ trials to all R&D [CLM-006032] [CLM-006034]; Lilly's TuneLab rests on data it estimates cost over USD 1bn [CLM-000280]. None has independent outcome evidence [CLM-011052]. Takeda has an agent platform and 63.4% workforce usage, both adoption measures [CLM-005058] [CLM-014043]. *(RECOMMENDATION)* BET-01 and BET-08.

**2. Next-generation R&D.** Every competitor AI-discovery deal found is at concept or prototype [CLM-011057]. Takeda's Nabla and Iambic collaborations carry success-based payments above USD 1bn and USD 1.7bn, payable only on data [CLM-006039] [CLM-006041]. AI-origin Phase 2 success is cited as about the same as conventional (secondary source) [CLM-006049]. Takeda's approved late-stage set is mostly licensed in, with orexin the in-house exception [CLM-005050] [CLM-005048]. *(RECOMMENDATION)* BET-03, BET-06, BET-07, BET-18.

**3. Robotics and autonomous science.** Scripted automation is a commodity service: Arctoris runs the former Lilly platform as a CRO [CLM-006013] [CLM-006016]. Owned or venture-funded labs ended in divestment or distressed sale [CLM-006052] [CLM-006051]. *(INTERPRETATION)* The Lilly-NVIDIA lab (up to USD 1bn, joint) can also be read as re-platforming at larger scale, so "owned labs fail" is weaker than first stated [CLM-000281] [CLM-006017]. *(RECOMMENDATION)* BET-09 rents; BET-10 stays at zero with triggers.

**4. Care models.** No company publishes adherence, persistence or time-to-therapy outcomes; reported figures are enrolment or access counts [CLM-007039]. Entyvio SC is approved in three regions and Takeda cites it as driving uptake; HYHUB received 510(k) clearance [CLM-007041] [CLM-007042]. Effect after biosimilar entry is unestablished [CLM-007053]. *(RECOMMENDATION)* BET-11, BET-12.

**5. Commercial models.** PBM template formularies decide biosimilar outcomes [CLM-007015]. Humira's contracting delayed but did not stop erosion [CLM-003007] [CLM-003002]. Takeda's contracting and patient-services evidence is a gap [CLM-007025] [CLM-005072]. A U.S. cash-pay channel fits infused and specialty-distributed products poorly [CLM-007052]. *(RECOMMENDATION)* BET-04, BET-16 rejected.

**6. Partnerships and ecosystem.** AbbVie runs an early-option stream of USD 0.6-1.0bn a year; Lilly expenses early purchases as IPR&D; J&J buys large commercial assets [CLM-009097] [CLM-009095] [CLM-009096]. Value destruction concentrates in neuroscience, gene and cell therapy and robotics [CLM-009100]. Takeda's pattern is one flagship purchase (Nimbus), then mid-size licences (Innovent, Protagonist) [CLM-009076] [CLM-009078] [CLM-012151]. *(RECOMMENDATION)* BET-06, BET-18; BET-15 rejected.

**7. People and operating model.** The thinnest domain in public evidence. J&J reviewed about 900 AI use cases and moved ownership to departments [CLM-006018]. Takeda targets over JPY 200bn gross annual savings by FY2028, at a restructuring cost series of about JPY 150bn, 170.0bn, then 160.0bn, with about 4,500 roles affected per press [CLM-005059] [CLM-005078]. The CEO changed on 24 June 2026 [CLM-005073]. *(RECOMMENDATION)* BET-17, with ownership off the CFO's savings target.

## 4. Sequencing logic

*(HYPOTHESIS argued from evidence)* Run the data standard and rented automation in parallel from 2027 H1, with a hard gate before any closed-loop (stage 3) work: the full data layer plus three triggers (an independent Lilly-lab result, Nabla first-in-human data, a measured CRO cost) [CLM-011066] [CLM-000281] [CLM-006039]. Plasma can seed its own data because nomogram data already exist at site level [CLM-005060]. The Entyvio chain has fixed dates and cannot slip: erosion scenarios, then the January 2028 posture, then the playbook live by 2028 H1 (v2), then the earliest entry window (ASM-025 widened to 2028-2032 in v2), then the 2032 patents [CLM-007026] [CLM-014051] [CLM-005017]. Licensing deals (BET-18) wait for IVB-012 and the leverage test.

## 5. What Takeda should not do

*(RECOMMENDATION)* Do not buy a USD 5bn+ asset on current leverage (BET-15). Do not build an owned autonomous lab (BET-10) or a TuneLab-style platform (BET-14). Do not launch a U.S. cash-pay channel (BET-16). Do not scale AI-design partnerships before first-in-human data (RM-020, 2028 H2). Do not roll out plasma automation before the baseline shows headroom (ASM-031). Do not read usage or training counts as value [CLM-014043] [CLM-006022]. Do not redesign patient programmes without a pilot: AbbVie reversed its loyalty redesign within months [CLM-003033]. Do not treat the USD 10-20bn peak range for six late-stage programmes as a forecast; it is not probability-adjusted [CLM-014049].

## 6. Where the strategy is weakest

*(INTERPRETATION)* The council rates no funded bet robust to pricing and biosimilar pressure (SCN-3). The Entyvio price from January 2028 is statutory and PBM private-label biosimilars route around contracts, so BET-04 holds value only as delay [CLM-007026] [CLM-007015]. Vyvanse, Trintellix and Takhzyro (patent to January 2036) are exposures the bets do not address [CLM-005015] [CLM-005016] [CLM-005019]. The probabilities behind the ranking (ASM-020, 021, 022, 028) are judgment.
