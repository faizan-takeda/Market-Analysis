## Comparison validity

- Rows 2, 3 and 5 count or describe items whose scale and accounting differ (see [rd_investment_and_productivity.md](rd_investment_and_productivity.md) and [partner_ecosystem_comparison.md](partner_ecosystem_comparison.md)). A count of approvals is not a measure of capability.
- Row 12 uses three different debt definitions. The registry holds a corrected label: the AbbVie and J&J "long-term debt (noncurrent)" series CLM-000066..000070 and CLM-000128..000132 are total long-term debt including the current portion (AbbVie 64,503 versus 58,941 non-current; J&J 41,438 versus 39,438 non-current in FY2025), while Lilly's series is the non-current line [CLM-000070] [CLM-000132] [CLM-000202]. Debt-to-revenue ratios use non-current amounts and are not leverage ratios; EBITDA is not available for the three U.S. companies in the registry (gap).
- Rows 9 and 10 are maturity statements and are detailed in [data_ai_maturity_matrix.md](data_ai_maturity_matrix.md) and [robotics_deployment_comparison.md](robotics_deployment_comparison.md).
- Row 11 records documented actions only; no influence or intent is inferred.
- Where the Takeda baseline classes a capability as an EVIDENCE_GAP (patient services, AI-enabled discovery, robotics), the gap concerns public evidence, not Takeda's internal capability [CLM-005072] [CLM-005080].
