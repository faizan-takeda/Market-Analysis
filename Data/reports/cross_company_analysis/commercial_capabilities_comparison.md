### 1.1 Price versus volume in the growth years

Lilly's 2025 revenue growth came from volume, partly offset by lower realised prices, mostly on Mounjaro and Zepbound. In Q4 2025 realised prices were 5% lower and volume 46% higher; in Q3 2026 realised prices were 15% lower and volume 53% higher, and the quarter included about USD 900m of one-off benefits (USD 410m of Jardiance milestones plus rebate and discount estimate adjustments) [CLM-007024] [CLM-014004] [CLM-014003]. J&J's Stelara decline cut Innovative Medicine growth by about 10.4 points in FY2025 [CLM-007017]. AbbVie's reported provisions for rebates and chargebacks (USD 65.1bn in 2025, 59.3bn in 2024, 56.8bn in 2023) show the scale of its gross-to-net, which none of the other three companies disclose in the sources read [CLM-007019].

### 1.2 Launch-execution evidence

- Supply or site capacity governed the pace of two launches: Carvykti ("share gains and capacity expansion") and Spravato (certified sites only) [CLM-007029] [CLM-007030].
- Kisunla reached USD 484m in the first nine months of 2026; a USD 21m Q1 2025 figure rests on a search snippet from a blocked source, and reasons for slow uptake are not established [CLM-001032] [CLM-007032].
- Takeda's U.S. business unit is focused on the Entyvio pen and on preparing oveporexton and zasocitinib launches; the Kim-era CEO said three launches fall within about 12 months of June 2026 [CLM-007033] [CLM-005074].

## 2. What is contested

- Whether cash channels take volume from insured patients: copay-accumulator and anti-kickback limits restrict combining discounts with insurance, and Lilly's "one in three new starts" is a new-start measure [CLM-007010] [CLM-007004].
- Whether PBM preference reflected net price (CVS says Wegovy's was lower) or leverage; Zepbound was re-added after about 11 months [CLM-007011] [CLM-007013].
- Whether TrumpRx volumes are material: Congressional Research Service reports listings and manufacturer counts, not sales [CLM-007009].
- Whether the Pear Therapeutics failure was reimbursement or product-market fit, which matters for any digital-therapeutic commercial model [CLM-007045] [CLM-007046].

## 3. What is unknown

- Self-pay share of Zepbound revenue, net price per prescription and gross-to-net for any company except AbbVie's provisions [CLM-007005] [CLM-007019].
- Field-force size, omnichannel metrics and hub-service performance for any company; adherence, persistence or time-to-therapy outcomes for any support programme [CLM-007039].
- Takeda's patient-assistance and market-access programme inventory beyond Help at Hand [CLM-005072].

## 4. Comparison validity

- Patient-programme figures are different units: Allē is loyalty members, LillyDirect is patients who accessed treatments, Help at Hand is cumulative free-medication recipients. They must not be compared [CLM-007036] [CLM-007004] [CLM-007037].
- Launch growth rates are not comparable across products at different life stages (Mounjaro in its third year versus Entyvio in its second decade) *(INTERPRETATION)* [CLM-007031] [CLM-007027].
- The only company-level evidence of commercial *execution quality* across all four is outcome revenue and formulary events; none discloses field-force or hub metrics, so no capability ranking is offered [CLM-007039].
