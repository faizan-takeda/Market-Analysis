## 2. Defined metrics and their validity

| Metric | Definition | Computable for | Valid for ranking? |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 R&D intensity | R&D expense ÷ revenue, as reported | all four (section 1.2) | **Partly.** Currency-neutral, but R&D definitions differ (AbbVie includes impairments; J&J includes MedTech; Lilly excludes acquired IPR&D; Takeda IFRS). Use for direction of travel, not level |
| M2 Window-cohort revenue share | FY2025 revenue of molecules first approved after 2021-11-21 ÷ FY2025 revenue | lower bounds for Lilly, J&J, AbbVie; not for Takeda | **No.** Penalises lifecycle growth (see [pipeline_and_revenue_dependence.md](pipeline_and_revenue_dependence.md)) |
| M3 Write-down ratio | audited impairment ÷ net price of the acquisition it relates to | AbbVie emraclidine only (54%) | **No.** One data point; other companies' window-era impairments have different bases (programme-level, not deal-level) |
| M4 Novel approvals ÷ cumulative R&D | approvals counted 2021-11 to 2026-11 against R&D 2022-2025 | counts exist; ratio rejected | **No**, see below |

**Why M4 is rejected.** (1) The approval window runs to November 2026 and includes seven approvals dated 2026 (Lilly's Foundayo and Onswik, J&J's Icotyde, AbbVie's Decnupaz and Juvmo, Takeda's Orzeyful and Mimrylo) that were funded largely by spending before and after the 2022-2025 window rather than by that spend alone [CLM-012006] [CLM-012007] [CLM-012012] [CLM-012016] [CLM-012017] [CLM-012019] [CLM-012020]. (2) Counts of 3 to 8 per company are too small for a ratio to mean more than noise *(INTERPRETATION)*. (3) The FDA novel list omits sponsor, and the sponsor of record for Jaypirca is Loxo Oncology, for Elahere ImmunoGen and for Epkinly Genmab, so some approvals are acquired or partnered assets rather than outputs of the acquirer's R&D spend [CLM-012001] [CLM-012013] [CLM-012014]. (4) AbbVie's revenue growth in the window rests on supplemental indications for Skyrizi and Rinvoq, not on novel approvals [CLM-012033] [CLM-012036]. (5) J&J's R&D includes MedTech, whose output is device clearances, not drug approvals [CLM-002004] [CLM-014026]. (6) Takeda's FY ends in March and its R&D is in JPY.

**Cumulative R&D over the four most recent fiscal years, for scale only** *(computed)*: Lilly USD 40.8bn, J&J USD 61.6bn, AbbVie USD 36.1bn (includes USD 4.5bn of impairment charged to R&D) and Takeda JPY 2,769bn. These cannot be set against each other without an FX basis and are listed only to show order of magnitude [CLM-000144] [CLM-000145] [CLM-000146] [CLM-000147] [CLM-000082] [CLM-000083] [CLM-000084] [CLM-000085] [CLM-000012] [CLM-000013] [CLM-000014] [CLM-000015] [CLM-005004] [CLM-003057].

## 3. Profit margins: comparison invalid as a ranking

- Lilly pre-tax income was 39.5% of FY2025 revenue (21.7% in FY2021) [CLM-001007].
- J&J pre-tax income USD 32,581m on USD 94,193m is 34.6% *(computed)*, but FY2025 benefited from reversal of about USD 7bn of talc reserve, and FY2024 pre-tax income was USD 16,687m (18.8%) after large litigation charges [CLM-000261] [CLM-002016] [CLM-000116] [CLM-000074].
- AbbVie pre-tax income USD 6,597m is 10.8% *(computed)*, depressed by USD 7.4bn amortisation and a USD 6.5bn change in fair value of contingent consideration [CLM-000055] [CLM-003040] [CLM-003041].
- Takeda reported operating profit fell to JPY 6.2bn after a JPY 403.5bn Amitiza provision; Core Operating Profit (company non-IFRS) was JPY 1,172.5bn, 26.0% of revenue *(computed)* [CLM-000272] [CLM-000273] [CLM-005014].

These are four different accounting bases (US GAAP with and without special items, IFRS with a company-defined core measure), so no margin ranking is offered *(INTERPRETATION)*.

## 4. What is contested

- Whether Lilly's falling R&D intensity reflects scale (denominator growth) or restraint: R&D spend itself roughly doubled (USD 6.9bn to 13.3bn), so the ratio fell because revenue grew faster [CLM-001006] [CLM-000143] [CLM-000147].
- Whether AbbVie's 2024 R&D peak is a research-spend signal: USD 4.5bn of it is an impairment [CLM-003057] [CLM-000270].

## 5. What is unknown

- Internal development cycle time, probability of success and cost per trial for any company; these are on Takeda's validation backlog (IVB-009) and absent for the competitors [CLM-005042].
- Lilly's own productivity metrics (A1 listed this as a gap) [CLM-001006].
- Whether Takeda's licence and acquisition spend sits in R&D, capitalised intangibles or both (gap) [CLM-009098].
