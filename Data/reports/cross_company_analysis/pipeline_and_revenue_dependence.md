## 2. Replacement-cohort revenue: a defined metric and why it is only partly computable

**Metric.** FY2025 revenue from molecules first approved by the FDA after 2021-11-21 as a share of FY2025 revenue (the "window cohort"). It measures how much current revenue comes from newly approved molecules.

| Company | Window-cohort revenue found in the registry | Share (lower bound) | Why it is a lower bound |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lilly | Mounjaro + Zepbound USD 36,507m [CLM-001003]; Kisunla USD 484m, Foundayo USD 281m (nine months 2026, not FY2025) [CLM-001032] | at least 56.0% of FY2025 revenue *(computed)* | Omvoh, Ebglyss, Jaypirca, Inluriyo FY2025 revenues were not extracted |
| J&J | Carvykti 1,887 + Tecvayli 670 + Talvey 463 = USD 3,020m [CLM-002024] | at least 3.2% *(computed)* | Imaavy, Inlexzo, Akeega and Rybrevant/Lazcluze components were not separable; Carvykti is licensed with a partner |
| AbbVie | Elahere U.S. USD 607m (global grew 43%) + Vyalev USD 482m [CLM-003036] [CLM-003037] | at least 1.8% *(computed)* | Epkinly revenue share and Emrelis not extracted; Skyrizi and Rinvoq were approved before the window and are not in the cohort even though they drive 42.3% of revenue |
| Takeda | none in FY2025; first approvals August 2026; Entyvio SC pen is a new presentation of an older molecule [CLM-012029] [CLM-012030] | not computable | Fruzaqla and Qdenga FY2025 sales not extracted (Qdenga JPY 40.8bn is approved outside the U.S. cohort definition) [CLM-005045] |

**Comparison status.** The metric is invalid as a ranking of R&D productivity because it favours companies whose growth comes from new molecules (Lilly) and penalises companies whose growth comes from lifecycle expansion of older molecules (AbbVie's Skyrizi and Rinvoq, J&J's Tremfya) [CLM-012147] [CLM-002053]. It is reported only to show that Lilly's recent revenue growth depends on a molecule approved inside the window while AbbVie's depends on molecules approved before it *(INTERPRETATION)*.

## 3. What is contested

- Whether Lilly's concentration is a vulnerability or the intended outcome: Lilly's CEO said GLP-1s are "probably 80% of the economic value" and framed R&D investment around that [CLM-014067]; the 10-K lists concentration, price concessions and IRA exposure as risks [CLM-001017] [CLM-001027].
- Whether Q3 2026 growth at Lilly is repeatable: Q3 2026 revenue included about USD 900m of one-off benefits and realized prices fell 15% while volume rose 53% [CLM-014003].

## 4. What is unknown

- Product-level revenue for Omvoh, Ebglyss, Jaypirca, Inluriyo, Imaavy, Icotyde, Epkinly, Fruzaqla (gap).
- Probability-adjusted peak sales for any pipeline asset; Takeda's range is explicitly unrisked [CLM-014049].
- A like-for-like count of Phase 3 assets: A12's snapshot lists 10 Lilly, 8 J&J, 6 AbbVie and 8 Takeda registry records, but the selection was curated rather than a census, so the counts are not a comparison of pipeline depth [CLM-012046] [CLM-012059] [CLM-012068] [CLM-012077].
