# Strengths and vulnerabilities by company

Cutoff 2026-11-21. "Strength" means a documented outcome or structure that distinguishes the company from at least one of the other three on evidence; it is not a score. "Vulnerability" means a documented exposure, not a prediction. Statements drawn from company releases record what the company said, not an outcome unless a filing or regulator record confirms it. Computed ratios are marked *(computed)*. Takeda is the baseline, so it is described on the same evidence rules.

## 1. Eli Lilly

**Strengths**
- Revenue grew 2.3 times between FY2021 and FY2025, from USD 28.3bn to USD 65.2bn (23.2% a year *(computed)*), against +19.6% (J&J excluding Consumer Health) and +8.8% (AbbVie) over the same years *(computed)* [CLM-000133] [CLM-000137] [CLM-002001] [CLM-000253] [CLM-000001] [CLM-000005]. J&J's like-for-like base is its FY2021 Pharmaceutical plus Medical Devices sales as recast, USD 78,740m *(computed)* [CLM-002001].
- Margin expansion alongside growth: gross margin 74.2% → 83.0%, pre-tax margin 21.7% → 39.5%, R&D intensity 24.5% → 20.5% while absolute R&D roughly doubled [CLM-001005] [CLM-001007] [CLM-001006] [CLM-000143] [CLM-000147].
- Capacity investment on a scale none of the others shows in the filed numbers: capex USD 1.3bn → 7.8bn, 12.0% of FY2025 revenue against 5.1% for J&J and 2.0% for AbbVie *(computed)* [CLM-000168] [CLM-000172] [CLM-000110] [CLM-000045].
- The only evidenced direct-to-patient cash channel and the only owned-compute and data-as-product pair among the four (outcomes not shown) [CLM-001042] [CLM-007002] [CLM-006004] [CLM-000280].
- Eight CDER novel approvals in the window, the most of the four [CLM-012000] [CLM-012007].

**Vulnerabilities**
- Concentration: tirzepatide 56.0% of FY2025 revenue, up from 15.6% in FY2023 [CLM-001003].
- Price: realised prices fell 5% in Q4 2025 and 15% in Q3 2026 while volume rose 46% and 53%; Q3 2026 included about USD 900m of one-off benefits [CLM-014004] [CLM-014003]. The 10-K lists price concessions under the November 2025 government agreement [CLM-001027].
- Payer leverage: CVS Caremark removed Zepbound for about 11 months before re-adding it [CLM-007011] [CLM-007013].
- Medicare negotiation reaches Trulicity and Verzenio (USD 9,999m of FY2025 sales in scope, at least 15.3% of revenue *(computed)*) from 2028 [CLM-008015] [CLM-000243] [CLM-000244].
- Capacity is mostly announced, not operating: of ten named sites only a few show operating evidence [CLM-014008] [CLM-014009] [CLM-014010] [CLM-014012] [CLM-014014] [CLM-014015].
- Debt rose from USD 15.3bn to USD 40.9bn (non-current) [CLM-000198] [CLM-000202].
- Litigation: 340B suits, insulin pricing multidistrict litigation, compounding [CLM-001069] [CLM-001018].
- AI and robotics programmes show no outcome; the remote robotic lab was divested [CLM-006007] [CLM-006013].

**Contested:** whether growth is repeatable given one-off Q3 2026 benefits [CLM-014003]. **Unknown:** self-pay share of Zepbound revenue; tirzepatide unit volumes [CLM-007005].

## 2. Johnson & Johnson

**Strengths**
- Most diversified revenue base: largest brand 15.2% of revenue; two operating segments of USD 60.4bn and USD 33.8bn [CLM-002073] [CLM-000253].
- Absorbed the Stelara decline (USD 10,361m → 6,078m in FY2025) while group sales grew 6.0%; excluding Stelara, sales grew about 12.3% *(computed)*, with about one point from acquisitions per J&J [CLM-000254] [CLM-000253] [CLM-002075].
- Myeloma depth: four brands total USD 17,371m, and Oncology grew 22.1% to USD 25,380m [CLM-002072] [CLM-002024].
- The only regulator records for AI-adjacent surgical robotics (OTTAVA De Novo, MONARCH 510(k), VELYS 510(k)s) [CLM-014022] [CLM-014025] [CLM-014026].
- Capacity to execute large acquisitions: USD 15.1bn (FY2024) and USD 17.5bn (FY2025) net of cash [CLM-009049].

**Vulnerabilities**
- Stelara is still falling: roughly a 690 basis-point drag on Q3 2026 Innovative Medicine growth, with CVS Caremark preferring biosimilars from July 2026 [CLM-002060] [CLM-007015].
- Talc: three bankruptcy attempts (two dismissed, one not confirmed), a USD 7.0bn charge in FY2023, a remaining reserve of about USD 3.4bn at FY2025 end, a proposed USD 5.5bn ovarian resolution (plaintiff counsel says USD 7bn or more is possible), and Q3 2026 net earnings of USD 210m after a special charge [CLM-002015] [CLM-002017] [CLM-002016] [CLM-002062] [CLM-002063] [CLM-002060].
- MedTech earns a 12.2% segment margin against 36.9% in Innovative Medicine, grew 2.5% in Q3 2026, and Orthopaedics grew 1.1% in FY2025; J&J announced the Orthopaedics separation [CLM-002004] [CLM-002060] [CLM-002029] [CLM-002037].
- Net debt rose from USD 2.1bn (FY2021) to USD 27.8bn (FY2025) after Abiomed, Shockwave, Intra-Cellular and Halda [CLM-002010] [CLM-002014].
- Device safety events: Impella Class I recall (66,390 pumps) and the Varipulse pause [CLM-002046] [CLM-002044] [CLM-014018].
- Pharma plus MedTech synergy is claimed by J&J and not evidenced in the registry; the margin gap and the separation point the other way [CLM-002074] [CLM-002004].
- Pipeline setbacks include aticaprant, the RSV vaccine, bermekimab and Laminar [CLM-002039] [CLM-002018] [CLM-002020] [CLM-009039].

**Contested:** whether Varipulse harm was device- or use-related [CLM-002045]; talc total cost [CLM-002062] [CLM-002063]. **Unknown:** installed bases of OTTAVA, MONARCH and VELYS; DARZALEX patent dates [CLM-006029].

## 3. AbbVie

**Strengths**
- Replaced a franchise that was 36.6% of 2022 revenue within three years; total revenue passed its 2022 peak in 2025 (USD 61,160m versus 58,054m) *(computed)* [CLM-003004] [CLM-003014] [CLM-003015].
- A contracting defence that kept at least 97% of adalimumab volume through March 2024 and then a co-branded Humira via Cordavis [CLM-003007] [CLM-003008].
- Settlement-based IP: no generic Rinvoq tablet expected before April 2037 [CLM-003021].
- A repeatable early-stage and option deal stream (USD 0.6-1.0bn of yearly upfronts) [CLM-009069] [CLM-009097].
- Skyrizi and Rinvoq growth continued: nine-month 2026 revenue USD 23.1bn combined; Q3 2026 revenue +10.3% [CLM-003023].

**Vulnerabilities**
- Concentration moved from Humira to Skyrizi and Rinvoq (42.3% of FY2025 revenue *(computed)*); Skyrizi biosimilar risk after 2033 has no disclosed settlement date [CLM-000266] [CLM-003021].
- Capital destroyed in neuroscience: emraclidine failed after the Cerevel deal and a USD 4.5bn impairment followed (54% of the USD 8.3bn net price *(computed)*) [CLM-003054] [CLM-003057] [CLM-009054].
- Aesthetics shrank (U.S. Botox Cosmetic -11% and Juvederm -15% in 2025), the 2029 expectation was cut from USD 9bn to north of USD 7bn, and a loyalty-programme redesign was reversed [CLM-003032] [CLM-003034] [CLM-003033].
- Four selected products face Medicare-set prices from 2026 to 2028 (at least 21.0% of FY2025 revenue in scope *(computed)*) [CLM-003080] [CLM-003025] [CLM-003028] [CLM-003030] [CLM-003031].
- GAAP earnings are depressed by amortisation (USD 7.4bn) and contingent-consideration changes (USD 6.5bn); net income was USD 4.2bn in 2025 [CLM-003040] [CLM-003041] [CLM-000030].
- Debt: USD 58.9bn non-current (USD 64.5bn including the current portion) [CLM-000070].
- Several Phase 3 setbacks (Venclexta VERONA, Epkinly overall survival, navitoclax) and device-related CRLs for ABBV-951 [CLM-012117] [CLM-012118] [CLM-012114] [CLM-012111] [CLM-012112].
- Humira antitrust class actions pending [CLM-003011].

**Contested:** causal share of the 2024 formulary change in Humira's decline [CLM-003010]. **Unknown:** Skyrizi and Rinvoq origin (licensed vs internal), AbbVie Ventures founding date, AI outcomes [CLM-009074] [CLM-006054].

## 4. Takeda (public baseline)

**Strengths**
- Plasma-derived therapies: 275 collection centres at FY2024 and immunoglobulin brands at 18% of revenue; no comparable franchise appears in the other three companies' evidence [CLM-005063] [CLM-005064] [CLM-005009].
- In-house orexin science produced oveporexton (FDA approval 2026-08-05), and the three Phase 3 readouts planned for 2025 were reported positive [CLM-005048] [CLM-005029] [CLM-005042] [CLM-005027] [CLM-005033] [CLM-005031].
- Two FDA approvals 23 days apart (August 2026) and an NDA under Priority Review for zasocitinib accepted 2026-09-14 [CLM-005029] [CLM-005032] [CLM-005035].
- Balance-sheet restraint on acquisitions: no significant acquisitions of businesses or assets in the three years to March 2026 [CLM-009087].
- Stop decisions executed despite write-down cost (cell therapy, soticlestat, ALOFISEL, EXKIVITY) [CLM-005025] [CLM-005024] [CLM-005022].

**Vulnerabilities**
- Entyvio is 21% of revenue and faces exclusivity loss in the latter half of the decade, patents to 2032, two FDA-accepted biosimilar BLAs, UK and Netherlands revocation actions, and Medicare negotiation from 2028 [CLM-005009] [CLM-005017] [CLM-014050] [CLM-014051] [CLM-008022].
- Revenue fell 1.7% (-2.7% at constant rates) in FY2025, U.S. revenue fell 9.0%, and H1 FY2026 revenue was -1.0% at constant rates; Vyvanse fell 52.0% in two years *(computed)* [CLM-005001] [CLM-005011] [CLM-005015].
- The AMITIZA verdict (USD 884.9m single damages, subject to trebling for two groups) drove a JPY 403.5bn provision and reported operating profit of JPY 6.2bn; Takeda plans to appeal [CLM-005013] [CLM-005014] [CLM-014047] [CLM-014048].
- Restructuring: about 4,500 roles affected; cost estimates of about JPY 150bn (March 2026), JPY 170.0bn (original FY2026 forecast) and JPY 160.0bn (revised, October 2026), with over JPY 200bn gross annual savings targeted by FY2028 [CLM-005078] [CLM-005059].
- TRINTELLIX generics after December 2026 [CLM-005016].
- The launch wave has to be proven: new-brand launch execution is classed POTENTIAL_NEW_CAPABILITY in the baseline; patient services, AI-enabled discovery and robotics are public-evidence gaps [CLM-005069] [CLM-005072] [CLM-005080].
- A CEO transition in June 2026 and one documented manufacturing quality failure (Hikari, 2020) [CLM-005073] [CLM-005020].

**Contested:** the USD 10-20bn peak range is unrisked and "not a forecast or target" [CLM-014049]. **Unknown:** internal probability of success, Entyvio erosion curve, Amitiza final judgement [CLM-005080] [CLM-005017] [CLM-014048].
