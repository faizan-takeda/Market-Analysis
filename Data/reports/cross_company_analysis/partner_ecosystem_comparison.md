### 1.2 Graph inventory counts (coverage, not a census)

The A9 ecosystem graph holds 49 evidence-linked edges: Lilly 12, J&J 10, AbbVie 18, Takeda 9. These counts show how many dealings A9 could evidence from filings and releases; they are not counts of each company's deals. J&J's 10-Ks list few licences with disclosed values, and Takeda's evidenced licence terms are thin, so the counts understate both [CLM-009049] [CLM-009087]. One edge value (Lilly-Morphic, 3,200) uses the headline figure rather than the audited charge, so the edge values must not be summed [CLM-009002].

## 2. Which partnerships create durable options

Durable here means the structure leaves the company with a right but not an obligation, over several years, at bounded upfront cost. On the evidence:
- AbbVie has the most option-type structures in the audited record, and its yearly early-stage upfronts run at USD 0.6bn to 1.0bn [CLM-009097] [CLM-009069].
- Takeda's Innovent arrangement combines a licence, equity and an option at USD 1.2bn upfront [CLM-009078].
- No option from the window has yet produced an approved product or an independent outcome, so durability is a *(HYPOTHESIS)* for all four companies.
- Lilly's only data-for-access structure (TuneLab) creates an option on partner data and models, with no uptake result [CLM-009026] [CLM-014056].

- Lilly: Catalyze360 bundles Lilly Ventures, Gateway Labs, ExploR&D and TuneLab; a USD 500m a16z fund is Lilly-funded; no stand-alone Lilly Ventures fund size was found; the CEO describes Gateway Labs as hosting "scale ups, not startups" and as an experiment that "may not work" [CLM-009025] [CLM-014063] [CLM-014068].
- J&J: JLABS was planned in 2016 at six sites with capacity for 225 companies; by August 2025 11 sites were active after exits from Toronto, Houston and Washington D.C., and a snippet says New York is closing, which would leave San Diego, San Francisco and Boston; no JJDC portfolio count is in primary sources [CLM-009051] [CLM-014064] [CLM-014065] [CLM-014066].
- AbbVie: AbbVie Ventures' founding is 2009 or about 2015 depending on the source (unresolved), and one source is sponsored content [CLM-009074] [CLM-009075].
- Takeda: no fund size or Center for External Innovation description in primary sources [CLM-014052].

## 4. Comparison validity

- **Deal values are not comparable across companies.** The registry holds announced totals, consideration transferred, net-of-cash amounts, expensed IPR&D and upfronts for different deals. Examples: ImmunoGen 10.1 / 9.8 / 9.2bn; Morphic 3.2 / 2.55 / 3.35bn; Lilly IPR&D expense 2,910m versus cash paid 3,008m for FY2025 [CLM-003053] [CLM-009002] [CLM-000182] [CLM-000177].
- **US GAAP versus IFRS.** Asset acquisitions are expensed as acquired IPR&D at Lilly and AbbVie, so the same economic purchase appears as R&D expense for them; whether Takeda capitalises is not established (gap) [CLM-009095] [CLM-009098].
- **Deal count is not capability.** A9's own tables count rows, and for Takeda eight rows meet its target only by counting write-offs and divestitures [CLM-009098].
- **Contract-term completeness differs.** Lilly and AbbVie terms come from audited notes; J&J and Takeda licence terms are thin or undisclosed [CLM-009049] [CLM-009087].
